## Full script

> **VO:** I'm the eldest daughter. I don't have a name. I have job titles.
> **MAMA:** Just hold him five minutes.
> **VO:** 6:45 a.m. Mama hands me my brother like a baton in a relay race. It was three hours.
> **VO:** 7:30. My sister needs help with maths. I don't know this maths. They changed maths. But eldest daughters can't say "I don't know." So I invented an answer. Confidently.
> **DAD:** The internet is broken.
> **VO:** The internet is fine. He typed his password into Google. Again.
> **VO:** Afternoon. Government office. I translate for Mama. Translating means making everything she says thirty percent more polite.
> **MAMA:** Tell him he's useless.
> **LULU:** She says thank you for your help!
> **VO:** Evening. My brother's crush left him on "seen." Now I'm a therapist. License: none. Fee: also none.
> **VO:** Mama's tired, so I cook. Everyone eats in silence, which in my family means "amazing."
> **DAD:** Too much salt.
> **VO:** Dad says "too much salt" about water.
> **VO:** Eleven p.m. My shift is over. For one beautiful moment, I'm just… me.
> **MAMA:** Lulu! You're awake? Your aunt is on the phone. She wants to know when you're getting married.
> **VO:** Four seconds. My break lasted four seconds.
> **MAMA:** What would we do without you?
> **VO:** …Okay. One more shift.

## Animation instructions
- **The tag slap is this episode's main animation**: the new tag drops from 160 % + 15° onto the chest (6 frames), 2-frame squash, tiny 4 px shake. Every new tag uses the same move, so the repetition becomes the rhythm.
- Shot 2: the baby is a separate layer that Mama's arm "passes": slide the baby layer from Mama's hand to Lulu's over 8 frames.
- Shot 5: the politeness meter fills from 0 to 30 % over the line.
- Shot 8: the tag peels: rotate the old tag 30° and drop it out of frame (8 frames), revealing ME.
- Shot 10: the hug = Mama's layer slides into Lulu's and both arms swap to P10.

## Virality
- **Why click:** "eldest daughter syndrome" is a known, widely shared identity in every culture.
- **Why keep watching:** the tag device makes viewers wonder "what's the next job?". It's a built-in curiosity loop every 7 s.
- **Punchlines:** see above; the translator beat (0:32) is the most clippable.
- **Audience:** women 16–35, especially from immigrant, Arab, South Asian, Latino and African families. Their siblings and mums also share it.
- **Shareable because:** "tag your eldest sister" / "send this to your mum", and the warm ending makes sharing it with family feel safe rather than mean.
- **Make the first 3 s stronger:** start on the second tag slap (EMPLOYEE #1) already in motion, and put "I don't have a name." as the very first caption.
