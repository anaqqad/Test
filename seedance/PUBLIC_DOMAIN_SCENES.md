# Best public-domain book scenes for 30 s reels

Rule of thumb (US): books published in 1930 or earlier are public domain. Use the BOOK's descriptions, never a
film's design (no Universal Frankenstein bolts and flat head, no MGM ruby slippers, no Disney looks).

Ranked by hook strength x how easy it is for AI to render (few people, one room, simple props).

Best series to start: horror (Dracula, Frankenstein, Tell-Tale Heart, Dorian Gray, Jekyll) gets the strongest hooks;
then "moral twist" classics (Les Miserables candlesticks, Jane Eyre wedding) that match the owner's best-performing
emotional formats.
