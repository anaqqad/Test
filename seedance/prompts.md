# Seedance 2.5 prompts

## 1. Rebuild of the original ("mother owns the house")

```
Vertical 9:16, 30s. Cinematic photoreal family drama, warm amber low-key interior light, soft afternoon sun through beige curtains, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, subtle film grain. Same 3 characters in every shot, identical faces and wardrobe, no other people.

Characters:
- MOTHER: Black woman, early 60s, shoulder-length grey-black curly natural hair, brown knit cardigan over mustard blouse. Sits on beige couch.
- SON: Black man, early 30s, short fade, trimmed beard, navy polo with white-tipped collar, blue jeans.
- WIFE: Black woman, early 30s, long straight dark-brown hair, cream silk blouse, black wide-leg trousers.

Location: modest suburban living room — beige sofa, wooden coffee table, table lamp, side table, framed family photos, white front door with sheer-curtained window. Blocking: Mother on sofa left; Son and Wife standing right, near the door. Screen direction never flips.

0s-1s: Wide establishing shot from behind the coffee table. Wife points at Mother. WIFE (cold): "I'm tired of having you here."
1s-3s: Over-the-shoulder from behind Mother's head, two-shot of couple. WIFE: "Pack your things and get out of this house."
3s-5s: Close-up Mother on sofa, hurt, pleading. MOTHER: "This is my home, too. Where am I supposed to go?"
5s-9s: Over-the-shoulder two-shot of couple. WIFE (dismissive): "I don't care. Get your things and leave. You don't belong here anymore." SON (flat): "She's right, Mom. You need to leave."
9s-12s: Close-up Mother, eyes wet, disbelief. MOTHER: "You're really choosing her over your own mother?"
12s-14s: Close-up Son, firm. SON: "Yes. This is my wife's home, too,"
14s-15s: Close-up Wife, smug half-smile. SON (off-screen): "and I'm standing with her."
15s-20s: Medium shot, Mother calmly picks up her phone, dials, raises it to her ear. MOTHER (calm, steady): "Hello? I'd like to report two people trespassing on my property."
20s-21s: Close-up Son, confused. SON: "Mom, what are you doing?"
21s-22s: Mother pulls a document from a manila folder. MOTHER: "Your father transferred this house"
22s-23s: Insert close-up of the property deed with an official seal, in her hand. MOTHER (V.O.): "into my name before he died."
23s-26s: Wide shot, Mother hands the folder toward the couple; Son looks down, stunned. MOTHER: "You and your wife just told the legal owner to leave her own house."
26s-27s: Wide shot, Mother rises from the sofa.
27s-28s: Medium close-up Mother, standing, confident slight smile. MOTHER: "So now you two can pack your things"
28s-29s: Close-up Wife, eyes wide, mouth open, shocked.
29s-30s: Wide shot, Mother points firmly at the front door. MOTHER: "and leave."

Camera: mostly locked-off with slow subtle push-ins on close-ups, hard cuts between shots. Audio: clear lip-synced English dialogue, quiet room tone, no music. No subtitles, no text overlays, no watermark.
```

## 2. Fresh variant: "The Deed" (new cast, kitchen, rain, new dialogue)

```

- ROSA (mother): Latina woman, late 60s, silver hair in a low bun, small gold hoop earrings, reading glasses on a chain, deep green wool cardigan over a white collared blouse. Sits at a kitchen table.

- CLAIRE (his wife): white woman, early 30s, shoulder-length blonde bob, red lipstick, camel blazer over a black turtleneck, gold necklace.

Blocking: Rosa seated at the table on the left; Daniel and Claire standing by the counter on the right. Screen direction never flips.

2s-4s: Over Rosa's shoulder, two-shot of the couple. CLAIRE: "It's time you found somewhere else."
4s-6s: Close-up Rosa, glasses lowered, stunned. ROSA (quiet): "Leave? I've lived here thirty years."
6s-9s: Two-shot of the couple. CLAIRE (sharp): "That's not our problem anymore." DANIEL (avoiding eye contact): "Mama, she's right. You have to go."
9s-11s: Close-up Rosa, eyes glistening. ROSA: "Daniel. You'd throw out your own mother?"
11s-13s: Close-up Daniel, jaw tight. DANIEL: "Claire is my wife. I'm with her."
13s-14s: Close-up Claire, small satisfied smirk.
14s-18s: Medium shot, Rosa calmly sets down her mug, takes out her phone, dials, raises it to her ear. ROSA (steady): "Yes, officer. Two people refuse to leave my home."
18s-20s: Close-up Daniel, confused laugh. DANIEL: "Mama, what are you doing?"

## 3. New format: "The Little House" (modest gift -> mocking friend -> grateful anyway -> luxury reveal)

Pattern from the owner's car idea (cheap car key -> friend mocks -> "he worked hard" -> key opens a Mercedes),
rebuilt with a house instead of a car.

```
Vertical 9:16, 30s. Cinematic photoreal feel-good drama, golden-hour suburban afternoon, warm low sun, lens flares, soft breeze in the trees, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, light film grain. Exactly 3 characters, identical faces and wardrobe in every shot, no other people. The video starts mid-moment on the first frame: no fade-in, no establishing shot.

Characters:
- MALIK (husband, early 30s): Black man, short twists, trimmed beard, warm nervous smile, faded denim work jacket over a white t-shirt, khaki work pants, scuffed tan work boots. Holds an old rusty key on a piece of string.
- AMARA (wife, late 20s): Black woman, big natural curly hair with a yellow headband, small gold studs, soft makeup, flowy white sundress, denim jacket, brown sandals. Warm and genuine.
- CHLOE (her friend, late 20s): white woman, long glossy blonde hair, oversized designer-style sunglasses pushed up on her head, beige luxury tracksuit, white sneakers, iced coffee, small quilted handbag with no logo. Snobby.

Location: quiet tree-lined street — a tiny worn wooden cottage with peeling pale-blue paint, a crooked porch, overgrown weeds and a squeaky wooden gate in the fence beside it; behind that fence, hidden from the street, a large modern white two-story house with floor-to-ceiling windows, a lit pool and palm trees. Blocking: the three stand on the cracked sidewalk facing the cottage; the garden gate is on the right. Screen direction never flips.

0s-2s: Close-up Malik, holding up the rusty key on its string, nervous hopeful smile. MALIK (speaking from the very first frame): "Babe… I bought us a house."
2s-4s: Wide shot, the tiny peeling cottage with its crooked porch and weeds; Amara and Chloe turn to look at it.
4s-6s: Close-up Chloe, snorting into her iced coffee. CHLOE: "That? That's a shed."
6s-9s: Close-up Amara, eyes filling with happy tears, taking the key in both hands. AMARA: "Really? Our own home?" She throws her arms around Malik.
9s-11s: Medium shot, Chloe rolling her eyes. CHLOE: "Girl, you deserve a mansion. Not this."
11s-14s: Close-up Amara, still holding Malik, smiling at Chloe. AMARA: "He worked double shifts for this. I love it."
14s-16s: Close-up Malik, deeply moved, a small knowing smile. MALIK: "Then come see the backyard."
16s-19s: Tracking shot, Malik leads them along the fence and pushes open the squeaky wooden gate.
19s-22s: Slow reveal, the camera glides through the gate: a large modern white house glowing in the golden light, the pool shimmering, string lights over a patio.
22s-23s: Close-up Chloe, sunglasses slipping off her head, jaw dropping. CHLOE: "Wait… that's yours?!"
23s-25s: Close-up Amara, hands over her mouth. AMARA: "Babe… what is this?"
25s-28s: Close-up Malik, grinning. MALIK: "The little one's the guest house. That key opens ours."
28s-30s: Wide shot, Amara leaps into Malik's arms and he spins her by the pool while Chloe stands frozen at the gate, iced coffee dripping from her tilted cup.

Camera: opening close-up, slow push-ins on close-ups, one smooth Steadicam move through the gate for the reveal, hard cuts. Audio: clear lip-synced English dialogue starting at 0.0s, birds and a light breeze, gate creak, soft acoustic guitar that swells into a warm, uplifting melody at the reveal. No subtitles, no text overlays, no watermark, no logos.
```

Hook text: "Her friend laughed at the tiny house he bought her… then he opened the gate 😳"
Caption: "She didn't care about the size. That's why she deserved it all. ❤️"

## 4. "Never Dance Alone" (hook-first: crying girl + strong man in the first 4 seconds)

Hook rule from the owner's past videos: the first 4 seconds decide everything. What works is opening on a
crying girl, or a strong man with a little girl running to him. This one does both by 0s-4s.

```
Vertical 9:16, 30s. Cinematic photoreal emotional drama, school gym at night decorated for a father-daughter dance: pink and gold balloons, string lights, a banner reading "FATHER-DAUGHTER DANCE", warm bokeh, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, light film grain. Main characters: exactly 4, identical faces and wardrobe in every shot. Background dads and daughters stay soft and out of focus. The video starts mid-moment on the first frame: no fade-in, no establishing shot, no title.

Characters:
- LILY (7): small white girl, strawberry-blonde hair in two braids with pink ribbons, freckles, tear-streaked cheeks, a pale-pink tulle dress slightly too big for her, white sparkly sneakers. Holds a tiny wrist corsage nobody gave her.
- BEAR (50s): huge, broad-shouldered white man, 6'5", shaved head, long grey beard, tattooed forearms and neck, black leather biker vest with no club logo over a crisp white dress shirt and a pink tie, dark jeans, black boots, a folded American flag patch on the vest. Gentle eyes.
- KAYLA (7): girl with a glossy black bob, sequined silver dress, holding her father's hand, smug.
- KAYLA'S DAD (40s): slim man in a tight navy suit, slicked hair, smirking.

Location: school gymnasium hallway and dance floor; double gym doors with push bars on the right; a wooden bench against the wall on the left. Screen direction never flips: Lily's bench left, gym doors right.

0s-2s: Extreme close-up Lily, alone on the bench, sobbing, lip trembling, a tear rolling down, the dance lights blurred behind her. LILY (whispering, breaking, from the very first frame): "Nobody came for me."
2s-3s: Hard cut, wide shot: the gym doors slam open, backlit, a giant tattooed biker silhouette fills the doorway. Heavy boots on the floor, every head turns.
3s-4s: Close-up Lily, she looks up, gasps, her face lights up through the tears. LILY: "Bear!" She sprints toward him.
4s-6s: Slow motion wide, Lily runs across the floor in her pink dress and leaps; Bear drops to one knee and catches her in a huge hug, lifting her off the ground.
6s-8s: Close-up Bear, eyes wet, his huge hand on the back of her small head. BEAR (deep, soft): "Sorry I'm late, princess."
8s-10s: Medium shot, Kayla tugging her dad's hand, pointing. KAYLA (loud, mocking): "That's not her dad. She doesn't have one."
10s-12s: Close-up Kayla's Dad, smirking, stepping forward. KAYLA'S DAD: "Sir, this dance is for real fathers."
12s-13s: Close-up Lily, hiding her face in Bear's vest, shrinking.
13s-17s: Close-up Bear, standing up slowly to full height, calm, quiet, towering over the man. BEAR: "Her father saved my life overseas."
17s-20s: Close-up Bear, jaw tight, voice cracking. BEAR: "Before he died in my arms, I promised him she'd never dance alone."
20s-21s: Close-up Kayla's Dad, the smirk gone, he looks down, ashamed.
21s-24s: Wide shot, the gym doors open again: a line of tough tattooed bikers in leather vests and dress shirts walk in behind Bear, each one holding a single pink rose.
24s-26s: Close-up Lily, mouth open, amazed. BEAR (off-screen): "And neither will any girl here tonight."
26s-28s: Medium shot, the bikers kneel and offer their roses to the other girls standing alone by the wall; the girls light up.
28s-30s: Wide shot under the string lights, Lily stands on Bear's big boots and they slow-dance, she laughs with tears still on her cheeks; Kayla watches, holding her dad's hand, silent.

Camera: opening extreme close-up, hard cut to the backlit doorway, slow motion for the run and catch, slow push-ins on close-ups, final slow crane up over the dance floor. Audio: clear lip-synced English dialogue starting at 0.0s, a sob on the first frame, a loud door slam at 2s, heavy boot steps, quiet gym murmur that falls silent when Bear speaks, soft piano that swells into a warm emotional string melody at the bikers' entrance. No subtitles, no text overlays, no watermark, no logos.
```

Hook text: "Nobody came to her father-daughter dance… until HE walked in 😭"
Caption: "A promise is a promise. ❤️🏍️"

All characters are original fictional people who do not resemble any celebrity, actor, TV or movie character. The video starts mid-moment on the first frame: no fade-in, no establishing shot.

an adult gripping a child (moderation risk), gravel vs asphalt mismatch, the dad's death only implied, no closing line.

```
