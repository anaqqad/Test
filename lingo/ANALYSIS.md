# Analysis: the 3 reference videos + @thatsquiteinteresting

## What the videos are
| video | length | segments | per language |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nearly Extinct Languages be like | 3:54 | 10 | 15-33 s |
| Insanely Niche Languages be like | 3:00 | 12 | exactly 15 s |
| Ranking languages by how useful they are | 4:00 | 20 | 12 s |

Uploaded copies were 640x360, 30 fps.

## The formula
1. **No intro, no narration.** The first frame is already the first language. Watch time is the whole game.
2. **One language = one screen.** A white title bar with the language name, the speaker count top-right
   ("<12", "1", "<2,300"), and a flag top-right in the niche video.
3. **A meme-style character** (wojak / "be like" art) in traditional clothing, bottom-left, over a stock photo
   of the region.
4. **2-4 short fact cards** pop in one by one (white boxes, small sans-serif text), so the viewer keeps reading.
5. **The audio is the language itself**: a recording of a speaker or a song ("You are currently listening to the
   only person who can speak this language"). That's the emotional hook, and why people watch to the end.
6. Ranking variant: black background, flag + name + score "9/10" and one paragraph. The order builds to a punchline
   (English 20/10).
7. Titles are memes ("… be like"), so the topic list is endless: extinct, niche, useful, hardest, weird sounds,
   regions, part 2, part 3…

## Weaknesses (where we beat it)
- 360p upload, blurry stock photos, tiny text that's hard to read on phones.
- Wojak art reused across countries; some characters are crude stereotypes.
- No map, so viewers don't know where the language is.
- Static frames: nothing moves except cards appearing.
- Facts are sometimes vague, and recordings are never credited.

## What LingoDude does better
- Real 1080p (4K possible: everything is vector), large readable text, consistent art style.
- A unique, respectful character per language and a matching illustrated landscape.
- A mini globe with a pulsing pin and a place label on every language.
- Motion: cards slide in, the character breathes, the background slowly pushes in, the score meter fills, and the
  ranking video builds an easy→hard axis with every flag so far.
- Word-by-word captions when the audio is a list of words.
- Every recording is real, free-licensed and credited (`out/*_credits.txt` → video description).

## Publishing tips
- Same title format as the niche leader: "Nearly Extinct Languages be like… (Part 2)".
- Thumbnail: one character + one flag + 2-3 huge words (see `out/brand/thumb_*.png`).
- Also cut each language into a 15-20 s vertical Short; these formats travel well on Shorts.
- Pin a comment asking "Which language next?" (the outro asks the same); viewer suggestions turn into Part 3.
