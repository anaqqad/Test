## Title
Languages Dutch Speakers Can Almost Understand

## Description
How much Dutch can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Six real speakers, each with a real link to Dutch: Brabantian from Helmond, Bildts from a village built on land won from the sea, Afrikaans from South Africa, Gronings, West Frisian, and Sranan Tongo from Suriname. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Dutch speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 6 in the comments 👇
