Languages Portuguese Speakers Can Almost Understand

## Description
How much Portuguese can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Six real speakers, each with a real link to Portuguese: Fala from three villages in Spain, Mirandese from north-east Portugal, Leonese, Spanish, Papiamento from Aruba, and Galician, Portuguese's closest relative. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Portuguese speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 6 in the comments 👇
