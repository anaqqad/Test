## Description
Part 2! How much English can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Five real speakers: Bislama from the Pacific, Afrikaans from South Africa, a poet from the Shetland Islands, Nigerian Pidgin, and Gullah from the Sea Islands of South Carolina. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

How much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 5 in the comments 👇 Missed Part 1? Watch it for Tok Pisin, Doric Scots, Frisian and more.
