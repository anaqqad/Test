Native languages of Mexico, Central and South America: Nahuatl, the language of the Aztecs that gave English 'chocolate' and 'tomato', tonal Otomi and Mixtec, Zapotec, the rainforest language Lacandon, K'iche' of the Popol Vuh, Caribbean-born Garifuna and Guarani, spoken by most of Paraguay. Every voice you hear is a real recording of the language.

Part 1 (Navajo, Cherokee, Ojibwe…) and Part 2 (Tlingit, Karuk, Comanche…) are on the channel. Which Native language should we add next? Tell us in the comments 👇
