## Description
Some languages are so close that their speakers chat without a translator. We ranked 9 pairs with a simple test: the same 10 everyday words, spoken by native speakers of each language. How many sound alike?

Every voice you hear is a real recording by a native speaker (Lingua Libre, via Wikimedia Commons).

HOW THE TEST WORKS
For each pair we picked 10 common words (water, house, bread, night...) and played the real recording in both languages. A word counts as alike when the two forms share the same origin and clearly sound similar. It is a quick test, not a scientific measure: it shows what you hear, not full mutual intelligibility.
