Tones aren't just an Asian thing. Part 3 goes to Africa and Mexico: Kpelle, Vai, Dagbani, Ibibio, Venda, Tsonga, Nuyoo Mixtec and Triqui, one of the most tonal languages on Earth. Watch the live pitch line draw every voice. Every voice you hear is a real recording of the language.

Part 1 (Mandarin, Thai, Yoruba…) and Part 2 (Cantonese, Hakka, Twi…) are on the channel. Which tonal language should we add next? Tell us in the comments 👇
