# YouTube metadata: Hear These Languages Before They Disappear (Part 2)

## Title
Hear These Languages Before They Disappear (Part 2)

## Description
Eight more languages with their last speakers: Ainu in Hokkaido, Miyako in Okinawa, Mendriq and Kensiu in the Malaysian forest, Lehali on one island of Vanuatu, Laz on the Black Sea, Northern Sami in the Arctic, and Bildts on land the Dutch took from the sea. Every voice is a real native speaker, recorded by Wikitongues and other volunteers and shared on Wikimedia Commons.

Part 1 (Wymysorys, Kusunda, Nuxalk, Cherokee, Vepsian, Lakota, Yugambeh, Ladino, Boruca): see the channel.
