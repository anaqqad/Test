## Description
Malta and Sicily sit 90 km apart. Both were ruled by Arabs for more than two centuries, and both were conquered by the same Norman count in 1091. Today Sicily speaks a Latin language, and Malta still says ktieb, the Arabic word for book. We put both islands on one clock to find out why, read the oldest known text in Maltese, and listen to the roots that are still there today.

Every Maltese and Arabic word you hear is a real recording by a native speaker. No AI voices for the languages.
