## Title
Ranking Languages by How Many Grammar Cases They Have

## Description
How many grammar cases does a language have? Cases are the endings that show who does what to whom: in Hungarian one ending turns "house" into "in the house", another into "out of the house". We ranked 11 languages from English with 0 to Hungarian with 18. You hear each one from a real recording of a speaker, and for Finnish, Estonian, Polish, Russian and English you hear one word change through its cases.

How we counted: the number of cases taught in each language's standard grammar. Linguists do not always agree. Hungarian is counted anywhere from 17 to 27, and some argue its endings are not cases at all. Languages where the counts disagree too much were left out (Tsez, Archi, Lezgian and Basque, which can reach dozens depending on the method).
