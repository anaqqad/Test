## Description
How much German can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Eight real speakers of languages related to German, from Luxembourgish and Swiss German to Dutch, Afrikaans and West Frisian, with the false friends that trick German speakers. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

German speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 8 in the comments 👇
