Tonal Languages be like… (Part 4)

## Description
You didn't know these were tonal! Swedish, Norwegian, Lithuanian, Latvian and Bosnian change a word's meaning with its melody, Punjabi has real tones from lost breathy sounds, and Dzongkha and Navajo use high and low tones too. Watch the live pitch line draw every voice. Every voice you hear is a real recording of the language.

Part 1 (Mandarin, Thai, Yoruba…), Part 2 (Cantonese, Hakka, Twi…) and Part 3 (Kpelle, Venda, Triqui…) are on the channel. Which tonal language should we add next? Tell us in the comments 👇
