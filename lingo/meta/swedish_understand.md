## Title
Languages Swedish Speakers Can Almost Understand

## Description
How much Swedish can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Seven real speakers, each with a real link to Swedish: Norwegian from Kristiansund, Åland Swedish from 1971, Danish, the famously hard Närpes dialect of Finland, Icelandic, a runestone read in Runic Swedish, and Old Norse, the language of the Vikings. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Swedish speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 7 in the comments 👇
