## Title
These Languages Break the Rules of Speech (Part 2)

## Description
Part 1 got a fair complaint: which rule, and where is the proof? So this time every language gets the rule it breaks and the exact word or sentence that breaks it, spoken by a native speaker. Eight consonants in a row in Dutch, a 67-letter German word, Arabic words built from a skeleton of three letters, three Vietnamese words that are the same syllable with a different tune, Japanese rain and candy told apart only by pitch, a Czech sentence with no vowel at all, a Polish syllable with nine letters, and the Icelandic volcano nobody could say.

Every recording is real (Wiktionary pronunciation files and Lingua Libre, via Wikimedia Commons).

Which rule did we miss? Tell us in the comments 👇
