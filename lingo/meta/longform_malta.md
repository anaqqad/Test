Arab Rule in Malta Ended in 1091. So Why Is Maltese Still Arabic at Its Core?

## Description
Sicily, Spain, Crete and the Balearic Islands all lost Arabic after Arab rule ended. Malta didn't. Catholic, European, in the EU, and yet its everyday words, its numbers and even its word for God come from Arabic. So who kept the language alive, and why did it survive when Sicilian Arabic vanished?

Every Maltese word you hear is a real recording by a Maltese speaker. No AI voices for the language.
