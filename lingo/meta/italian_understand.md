## Description
How much Italian can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Twelve real speakers: Neapolitan, Sicilian, Lombard, Sardinian and Friulian from Italy itself, and Spanish, Catalan, French, Portuguese, Romanian, Romansh and Occitan from its neighbours. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Italian speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 12 in the comments 👇
