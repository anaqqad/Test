# YouTube metadata: Language Isolates be like… (Part 2)

## Title
Language Isolates be like… (Part 2)

## Description
Part 2! Language isolates are languages with no known relatives anywhere on Earth. These five are related to nothing else, and every voice you hear is a real speaker: a Purépecha teacher from Mexico, a Ticuna speaker from the Amazon, a story told by a Hadza hunter-gatherer in Tanzania, a Haida clapping chant from Canada, and a Karuk conversation lesson from California.

Missed Part 1 (Burushaski, Mapudungun, Sandawe, Warao, Tiwi)? It's on the channel.

Which language isolate should we cover next? Tell us in the comments 👇
