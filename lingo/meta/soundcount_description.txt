How many different sounds does a language use? We counted the consonants, vowels and tones of 13 languages and ranked them, from Pirahã with just 11 to !Xóõ (Taa) with 161, most of them clicks. Every voice you hear is a real recording of a speaker.

How we counted: linguists have published sound inventories for thousands of languages, and different studies don't always agree. For each language we took the middle value of all the studies. Languages where the studies disagree too much were left out (Japanese, Spanish, Finnish, Hawaiian, Hungarian and Hindi range from about 13 to 94 depending on the study). English and German tie at 40.

Which sound in your language is hardest for learners? Tell us in the comments 👇
