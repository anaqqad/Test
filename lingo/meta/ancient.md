About the voices: nobody alive has heard these languages. Every voice in this video is an AI simulation: open-source Piper text-to-speech voices reading scholarly reconstructions of the pronunciation, written in IPA. Experts disagree on many details, especially vowels, so treat it as a best guess, not a recording.
Texts: Hávamál 76, Beowulf 1-2, Gothic Bible (Matthew 6:9), Caesar, Homer's Iliad 1.1, Hrozný's Hittite sentence, Code of Hammurabi law 1, the Egyptian royal formula ankh wedja seneb, the Sumerian King List, and Schleicher's fable in the version by Andrew Byrd.
