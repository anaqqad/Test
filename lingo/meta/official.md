How we counted: every country defines "official" differently, so each number follows the country's own constitution or law.
Switzerland: 4 national languages (Federal Constitution, art. 4).
Singapore: 4 official languages (Constitution, art. 153A).
South Africa: 12 official languages, including South African Sign Language since 2023 (Constitution, section 6).
Zimbabwe: 16 officially recognised languages (Constitution of 2013, section 6).
India: 22 scheduled languages (Eighth Schedule of the Constitution); Hindi and English are the official languages of the central government.
Bolivia: 37 official languages, Spanish plus 36 indigenous languages (Constitution of 2009, article 5).
