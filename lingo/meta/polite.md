## Title
Ranking the World's Most Polite Languages

## Description
Which language has the most ways to be polite? We counted every word the dictionary labels polite, honorific or humble, from Chinese "your precious surname" to the Thai words used only for the king, then ranked the top 10. Every polite word you hear is a real recording by a native speaker.

How we ranked: the number of words labelled polite, honorific or humble in the English Wiktionary for each language (plus Thai royal vocabulary), counted in October 2026. "Formal" words were left out, because formal style is not politeness. The count measures polite words, not polite grammar, so languages like Korean, whose politeness lives in verb endings, are underrated. Tibetan (48 words) would be #8, but we found no free recording of a Tibetan polite word, so it was skipped.

Which language is the most polite? Tell us in the comments 👇
