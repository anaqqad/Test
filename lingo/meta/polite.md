# YouTube metadata: Ranking the World's Most Polite Languages

## Title
Ranking the World's Most Polite Languages

## Description
Which language has the most ways to be polite? We counted every polite, honorific, humble and royal word in four dictionaries, removed duplicates and set phrases, and ranked the top 10. From the Japanese humble verbs to the Thai words used only for the king, every polite word you hear is a real recording by a native speaker.

How we ranked: words labelled polite, honorific, humble or royal, merged from four dictionaries: the English Wiktionary (every language), the Japanese Wiktionary (honorific language of Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Javanese, Tibetan and Vietnamese), the Korean Wiktionary (honorific and humble words of Korean, Japanese, Thai and English) and the Thai Wiktionary (Thai royal vocabulary), counted in October 2026. A word found in several dictionaries counts once; Chinese traditional and simplified spellings, and Japanese kanji and kana spellings, are merged. Only single words count: phrases, idioms and set expressions are removed. "Formal" words are left out, because formal style is not politeness. The count measures polite words, not polite grammar, so languages whose politeness lives in verb endings, like Korean, are underrated. Khmer (81 words) and Tibetan (48) would rank #8 and #9, but we found no free recording of a single polite word in them, so they were skipped.

Which language is the most polite? Tell us in the comments 👇
