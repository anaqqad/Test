Some languages glue ending after ending onto one word until a single word is a whole sentence. Turkish 'Çekoslovakyalılaştıramadıklarımızdanmışsınız' means 'you are apparently one of those we couldn't make Czechoslovakian', Swahili says 'I will love you' in one word, and Greenlandic builds whole sentences out of one verb. Watch each word built piece by piece. Every voice you hear is a real recording of the language.
