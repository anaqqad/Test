## Title
Weirdest Words in Made-Up Languages be like

## Description
People invented these languages from scratch, and then they invented words no natural language has. Esperanto has a verb for speaking your own language at an Esperanto meeting (it means "to crocodile"). Toki Pona has about 120 words and spends one of them on its own textbook. Lojban turns feelings into tiny sounds. Láadan was built to name experiences other languages ignore. Each word is said by a real speaker, then English tries to keep up, ranked by how many English words it takes.

Every made-up word is a real recording by a speaker on Lingua Libre (via Wikimedia Commons). The English translations are read by an AI voice, labelled on screen.

Which made-up word did we miss? Tell us in the comments 👇
