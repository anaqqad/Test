Languages English Speakers Can Almost Understand (Part 1)

## Description
How much English can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Five real speakers: a creole from Guyana, a rare creole spoken in Texas and Mexico, Doric Scots from north-east Scotland, Tok Pisin from Papua New Guinea, and Frisian, English's closest relative on the continent. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

How much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 5 in the comments 👇 Part 2 is coming with more languages.
