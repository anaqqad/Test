# YouTube metadata: Whistled Languages be like (Part 2)

## Title
Whistled Languages be like (Part 2)

## Description
Part 2 of the languages that need no voice. Four more peoples who talk by whistling: the Chinantec of Oaxaca whistling through the fog, Hmong farmers in the forests of Laos, a Pirahã hunter in the Amazon telling his friend where the monkey is, and two Wayãpi hunters in French Guiana planning a canoe trip. Every whistle is a real recording, and the on-screen lines are what is actually being whistled, taken from the original films.

Part 1 (Silbo Gomero, Kuşköy bird language, Sfyria, whistled Occitan and whistled Yoruba): see the channel.
