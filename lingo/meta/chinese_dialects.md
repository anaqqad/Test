## Title
How Different Do Chinese Dialects Sound?

## Description
"Chinese" is not one way of speaking. Listen to eight real speakers from four branches of the Chinese family, then see what they said: Mandarin from Beijing, Henan, Guiyang and Yunnan, Wu from Shanghai and Linhai, Yue from Guangdong, and Min (Hokkien) from Penang in Malaysia. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Chinese speakers: which one could you understand? Tell us in the comments 👇
