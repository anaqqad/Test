## Description
How much Turkish can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Five real speakers, from the mountains of Kyrgyzstan to Azerbaijan, every one a Turkic language, from the hardest for a Turkish speaker to the easiest. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Requested by a viewer (thank you @arpetr3458)! Turkish speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 5 in the comments 👇
