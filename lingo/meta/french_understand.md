Languages French Speakers Can Almost Understand

## Description
How much French can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Eight real speakers, each with a real link to French: the Norman of Jersey and Normandy, Picard (the Ch'ti of the north), three French-based creoles from Louisiana, Haiti and Réunion, and France's southern neighbours Occitan and Catalan. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

French speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 8 in the comments 👇
