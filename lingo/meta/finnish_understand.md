## Title
Languages Finnish Speakers Can Almost Understand

## Description
How much Finnish can you hear in these languages? Listen first, then see the translation. Seven real speakers: Estonian, a Finnish that was exiled to Siberia in 1802, Livvi Karelian, Võro from south-east Estonia, Ludic with about 300 speakers, Veps, and Hungarian, Finnish's very distant cousin. Every voice is a real recording, no AI.

Finnish speakers: how much did you understand? Tell us your score out of 7 in the comments 👇
