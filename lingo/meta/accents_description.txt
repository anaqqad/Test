Which language uses the most accent marks? We counted every letter with an accent mark (á, č, ő, ñ…) in each official alphabet and ranked the top 10, from Spanish to Vietnamese. Every word you hear is a real recording by a native speaker. Thanks to the viewer who suggested this one!

How we counted: lowercase letters with accent marks in the standard alphabet. Letters like ß, þ, æ or œ are not counted, because they are separate letters or ligatures, not accented ones. Vietnamese has 67: its 12 vowels can each take 5 tone marks, plus ă, â, ê, ô, ơ, ư and đ. Polish, Lithuanian and Hungarian all have 9; Polish and Lithuanian use 4 different kinds of marks and Hungarian 3, so Hungarian comes 9th.
