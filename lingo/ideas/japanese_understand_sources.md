Yonaguni videos are CC BY-NC or copyrighted (not usable on a monetised channel). So the clips come from YouTube videos
marked **Creative Commons Attribution (reuse allowed)**, each licence checked on its watch page on 2026-11-25.
YouTube blocks downloads from the cloud session ("confirm you're not a bot"): download on the owner's PC with
`bash lingo/fetch_youtube_cc.sh` (needs yt-dlp and ffmpeg), then send the files from `lingo/incoming/japanese/`.
