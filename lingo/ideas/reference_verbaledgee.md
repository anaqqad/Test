## Ideas for LingoDude (language angle, English as the reference point, real recordings where there is sound)
1. How People Laugh in Texts Around the World: lol / jajaja (Spanish) / mdr (French) / kkkk (Brazil) /
   555 (Thai, 5 = "ha") / www (Japanese) / ㅋㅋㅋ (Korean) / xaxa (Greek). Short or long.
2. Gen Z Slang in Other Languages: how teens say "cringe", "bro", "no cap", "slay", "rizz" in Spanish,
   French, German, Japanese, Korean, Arabic.
3. English Slang That Came From Other Languages: "bro"... "boss" (Dutch baas), "guru" (Sanskrit),
   "kudos" (Greek), "ninja" (Japanese), "schmooze" (Yiddish), "ok" history. Real speakers say the originals.
4. Texting Abbreviations Around the World (Part 1, 2...): French "stp", "jsp"; Spanish "q", "tqm";
   German "hdl"; Arabic Arabizi numbers (3 = ع, 7 = ح).
5. Millennials vs Gen Z vs Gen Alpha... in Another Language (Spanish or French slang by generation).
6. Words With No English Translation (their "feelings you didn't know had a name" did well as a format):
   hygge, saudade, schadenfreude, wabi-sabi, tartle, gigil. Already item 25 in TODO.md.
7. Comebacks/insults: only the safe version, e.g. "The Most Polite Insults in Other Languages" (no slurs).
