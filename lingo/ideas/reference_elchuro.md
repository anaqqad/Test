## Format (from the titles and thumbnails)
- **"How [X] sounds"**: one region or family, many varieties, real speakers, compared side by side on a map.
- Often the **same sentence or the same clip** in every variety, so the differences are easy to hear.
- Titles: "How Different Do … Sound", "Hear the Many Languages of …", "What … Sounds Like", "How Far Does … Reach".
- Thumbnail template:
  - black background;
  - a dark map of the region with the area lit up (red or colours);
  - big caps text, white or red ("ONE SENTENCE / 20 MAYAN LANGUAGES", "NOT JUST RUSSIAN", "SPEAKS DIFFERENTLY");
  - a real person (a speaker or a news presenter) or a familiar clip on the right.
- Series with parts (Chinese cities 1 and 2; America atlas part 2).
