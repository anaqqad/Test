# Series idea: one whole video about one language (owner, 2026-10-29)

Working title format: "<Language> be like…" or "<Language> Explained in 3 Minutes". 2–4 min, real recordings only.

Segments (pick 6–8 per video; each is a reusable template so every episode is quick to build):
1. Hook: one surprising fact + the language's own name for itself, spoken by a native speaker.
2. Where it is spoken: map zoom (maptour engine) with speaker count and countries.
3. How it sounds: 15–20 s of a real native speaker (Wikitongues / Lingua Libre / Creative Commons).
4. Family tree: its relatives (or "no relatives at all" for isolates), drawn as a small tree.
5. Writing system: the alphabet or script, with one letter or character that does not exist elsewhere.
6. Count to 5 (or 10): real recordings of the numbers, captioned.
7. 3–5 words with no English equivalent, each spoken (Lingua Libre) with the literal meaning.
8. The hardest part for English speakers (cases, tones, clicks, gender...).
9. Words English took from it (e.g. Hungarian "coach" from Kocs, Finnish "sauna").
10. Is it safe or endangered? A meter like the UNESCO status.
11. Outro: "Would you learn <Language>?" + next episode teaser.

Good pilots (plenty of real recordings and strong hooks): Hungarian, Basque, Georgian, Icelandic, Finnish, Welsh,
Turkish, Swahili, Maltese.
