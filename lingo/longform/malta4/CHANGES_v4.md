## Sound design
- Separate beds per location: harbour and street footage keep their own sound; the table sits on a paper-and-room bed; the board on a quiet pencil bed; the manuscript on a paper bed with a page turn as it enters; the bakery on its own oven and work sound.
- Each bed enters 0.6 s before its picture (the next place is heard first); Foley on every event: coin on wood, paper, tack, pencil, door.
- Score changes texture per sequence, is muted under every native word, and drops out for discoveries ("On Malta, people are still speaking Arabic.", the poem's first line).
- Mix: −16.2 LUFS, true peak −1.6 dBTP; phone-speaker simulation keeps 93 % of the narration intelligible and every native word ≥ 6 dB over the bed.
