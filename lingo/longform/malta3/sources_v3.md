## Recordings
Every Maltese or Arabic word in the film is a real recording (Lingua Libre / Wikitongues), credited in
`out/longform_malta3_credits.txt`. Missing: **no native recording of ktieb inside a natural sentence** exists on Commons /
Lingua Libre. The film resolves the opening with the single-word recording plus Vassalli's printed title, and uses the
real proverb recording (s7) as its natural-phrase moment. A recorded phrase (e.g. "Qed naqra ktieb") from a Maltese
speaker would replace r4.
