What the numbers say so far (re-check as the 30-min episodes age; views at 1–2 days mean little):
- The two hits are contrasts about **enemy POWs inside America**, with an expectation the country upended: "were told to
  die → baseball" (52K), "no fence → knocked on a teacher's door" (9.5K). Runners-up are the same shape (trains, nurses,
  hid 40 years, sukiyaki: 1.4–1.9K).
- Stories about crimes, trials and executions on the home front mostly did worse (Seattle 114, Detroit 402, Krauss 471;
  the Salina massacre, 991, is the exception).
- Too early to judge the 30-min Pacific/MacArthur run (Navajo, Los Baños, Homma). Compare them at 2 weeks with the
  short German-POW episodes at the same age, and check average view duration in Studio: a 30-min video is worth more watch
  time per view.
- Thumbnail CTR clue (9 Nov): face + short question in the POW's voice beat fact statements (baseball 4.1 % vs trains 3.1 %).

## Lessons by generation (newest last; each is now a rule in the skill)

- **Blueprint 1, 10-min episodes (1–22):** competitor-first topics; one concrete surprising hook scene; "no source, no
  line"; best-quality archive copies (never 512 kb); QC every shot at 1 fps for symbols, slates, flash frames, title cards,
  wrong faces; never label an American as German; stand-ins tagged.
- **Fort Lawton (14):** a shot plays on after its in-point; check the whole length (grave cross with a name, FDR's face, a
  white face under a Black veteran's name).
- **Video quality (10 Nov):** owner said videos looked bad: 1080p cards and clips (`--hd`, HEIGHT=1080), pick sharp films.
- **Cherbourg (17):** first 1080p build.
- **Palawan (21):** owner wanted "much more hooking": cold open on the alarm, open loops, 7 highlighted documents.
- **Wainwright (22):** use the winning competitor's title format literally; one title, one thumbnail, no A/B.
- **Navajo (23), first 30-min:** owner: length matters most, at least 25 min; vary card styles (globe maps, quote cards,
  desk documents, archive prints), not all ledger cards.
- **Los Baños (24):** four desk documents squashed (DocDesk aspect = h/w); re-uploaded. render.mjs now skips a failing
  card; card contact sheet before assembling. Disk: delete clips once parts exist.
- **Homma (25):** built from the 3,365-page trial record (archive.org full-text search for page numbers); first original
  survivor voice spliced in (`[CLIP]` lines); Fox newsreel timecode cropped and dark reels graded; `risk.py` ranges for
  slates and flashes. Studio: thumbnail only stuck via synthetic drag-and-drop; captions stayed a draft until published.
  Mistake: clicked "Publish anyway" before YouTube's checks finished — wait next time. Owner feedback: quote cards too
  small and off-centre → now 76 px, dead centre.
- **Cabanatuan (26):** four original voices (Leyte prisoner twice, Cpl. Hay, Sgt. Goldie), the first in minute 1; 8 time
  segments. Disk dips (page file + other renders) froze one card render and **truncated** a webm that still opened
  (3.2 s of 7.8 s): compare every overlay's duration with end − start before assembling. `tools/plan.py` is built from
  `build/plan_*.py` — editing plan_body.py alone changes nothing. `make_music.py` ran out of memory at 1,828 s: make 960 s
  and loop it with ffmpeg. Card text "At 1945" read as a year → "At 1945 [7:45 p.m.]". Studio: the upload dialog's
  thumbnail input worked this time; end screen = "1 video, 1 subscribe" template → Choose specific video.

- **Bush / Chichi Jima (27):** first famous-name episode (Nobody To Legend's "<Branch> File Shows What <name> Really Did"
  title, Restricted Files devices on our blueprint: three-claim myth loop paid off by a `Verdict` stamp card, FROM THE FILE /
  AS HE TOLD IT / Stand-in tag prefixes, `thumb_declassified.py` thumbnail). Bush's own voice five times, the first at 1:57.
  Wikimedia originals answer 429 to curl: serve a localhost receiver, open it in the built-in browser, `fetch` the Commons
  file in the page and POST it back. Microfilm scans the OCR can't read: `tools/line_bands.py` measures the typed lines.
  Plan anchors must avoid words the TTS respells (PRON names, years): they never match `words.json`. A QuoteCard whose
  attribution phrase is the same as its line crashes Remotion (`inputRange [28,24]`): put `byAt` on a later phrase.
  Tags that overlap any card are dropped silently, so a stand-in tag right after a print vanished: put it on the next
  shot. Overlay ids shift when a card is added: rename the old webms by content instead of re-rendering 100 cards.
  Studio: youtube.com/upload jumped to another channel; open `studio.youtube.com/channel/<id>/videos/upload` and switch
  accounts at youtube.com/channel_switcher.

- **Missouri surrender (28):** WWII Box's MacArthur format on a trending topic (other channels' Missouri videos 165K-420K
  that week), thumbnail copied exactly (straight red arrow). First episode with **3D reconstructions** (owner, 23 Nov:
  Immersive Past's 1.4M video is about half simple 3D): 8 Blender EEVEE shots, faceless clay figures. Lessons: EEVEE
  motion blur cost ~10 s/frame (off: ~2 s); PNG frames filled the disk (JPEG); a killed Blender let the loop encode a
  short clip that the next run skipped (encode only after a clean exit); `TaskStop` leaves bash render loops alive and
  editing the script under them mixes versions (kill by command line, write a new file). Four real MacArthur clips from
  government reels (Navy Combat Bulletin No. 7, United News 208-UN-171B); his best lines are on no reel, so quote cards.
  Kokoro read "Shigemitsu" as "Shijemitsu": "Shee-gay-meet-soo" (whisper-checked). Cards: three parallel `render.mjs`
  processes. **Any plan change after cards render shifts overlay ids**: copy storyboard.json first and rename webms by
  content (did it twice). QC found NARA 80-G "photos" that are index cards, United News eagle end cards, white flashes,
  and two Screen Magazine shots not from Japan: QC every clip at start/mid/end before assembling.

- **Cowra breakout (29):** back to our winning "Japanese POWs…" title shape after the MacArthur / Pacific run flopped
  (12-50 views each). Owner feedback on Missouri became rules: **no 3D second on screen more than twice** (14 shots, five
  of them second angles; `tools/reuse_check.py`) and **documents full frame on their own** (`LedgerDocument plain: true`,
  no card template). QC found the same rule broken by **archive film**: the last 4 minutes looped 36 s of one Noumea
  reel (up to 6x) and the night inserts reused NB 386-392; `reuse_check.py` now counts every film, visible time only, and
  the fix used free ranges from a contact sheet of unused seconds. The card sheet also caught photos repeated 3-4 times
  (Toyoshima's ID photo 4x, the same Sunday Telegraph front page 3x, one Hardy print twice back to back): count
  ArchivePrint images before rendering. BigNumber labels over ~55 characters wrap into the source line; GlobeMap pins
  that sit close need `"below": true` (new) on one label. Episode data lives in tools/ (Missouri's build/ scripts were
  lost). Blender: night needs exposure ~1.1 and moon 0.25; paths must be absolute (`$(pwd -W)`) or frames land in C:uild.
  AWM gives 640 px downloads only. Shared PC: cards and assembly ran on the queue (#7); small CPU jobs of others alongside.
  Owner after publishing ("what does this place mean", 18:20): the `ash_walk` shot (burnt hut floors at dawn) read as
  dark slabs on a grey void: flat grey sky, grass rendered grey, smoke too thin to see, no hut or fence for scale, and it
  ran over "the shooting went on" while the words that explain it ("almost every hut was ash") came 6 s later over film.
  Rule: **every 3D shot must say what it is without the tag**: one recognisable object (a half-burnt hut, a fence, a
  person), colour and sky that are not grey, and it sits on the sentence that names it. Check each 3D shot's middle
  frame with the sound off before assembly: if you cannot name the place, re-render.
  Then the owner: "i said before don't use 3D rendering anymore": **no Blender/3D reconstruction scenes from now on**;
  unfilmed moments get photos, tagged film stand-ins, maps, documents and quote cards.

- **Lunchroom POWs (30):** owner switched to **15-min episodes** and **Captive Witness's titles and thumbnails** (25 Nov
  2026) after the 30-min run got 20-466 views; topic = the documented version of the niche's "German POWs were treated
  better than..." trend (Trimmingham's Yank letter, Lena Horne at Camp Robinson, Lloyd Brown in Salina). Thumbnail: B&W split
  on real images + 2-word yellow question ("THE KITCHEN?"). No 3D. QC found: Film Bulletin 164 is too faded to use even
  graded (white blobs) and 'The Negro Soldier' mixes in Pearl Harbor ruins, Chinese refugees and combat under unrelated
  lines: **check every shot's content against its sentence, not just symbols** (sheet each film's used ranges first).
  Kokoro and Whisper here run on the CPU (no GPU slot needed). PC queue: Restricted Files coordinates; ask it before every
  heavy job and report "done". Never delete published episodes' out/*.mp4 (skill rule; Cowra's was deleted by mistake).
