## 1. Current affiliation, lab, roles and links

### Takeaway
Beuran is an Associate Professor (since 2022) who runs the "Cybersecurity Research Lab" in JAIST's Next-Generation Digital Infrastructure (NGDI) area. Its code lives at github.com/cyb3rlab. The older tools are at github.com/crond-jaist, the org of the CROND NEC-endowed chair, which closed in March 2021. Tan heads JAIST's "Laboratory on IoT and Smart Systems" (NGDI), which is the smart-home/ECHONET side. He is still a co-author on Beuran-lab papers published in 2026. No source found confirms emeritus status.

## 2. Beuran's software catalogue (cyber ranges, training, CPS testbeds, RL/LLM)

### Takeaway

- 2017–2020: cyber-range and training tools (CyRIS, CyTrONE, CyLMS, CyPROM, IoTrain)
- 2020–2022: smart-grid simulation (GridAttackSim, GridAttackAnalyzer) and DRL pentesting (AutoPentest-DRL)

- 2024–2026: LLM security/CTI work (RAF-AG, CyLLM-DAP, CodeEnhancer, LLMSafeguardEval-NonNL), smart-building emulation (SBCSE) and multi-agent cyber defense (CMARL-ACD)

Every crond-jaist repo is frozen; their last code activity was mostly the 2022-02-15 "license text" commit.

## 6. Evolution of Beuran's research programme

### Takeaway
Beuran's career follows one methodological thread: building realistic, reproducible experimental environments. It runs from network-quality measurement (PhD, CERN) to wireless network emulation on NICT's StarBED (QOMET/QOMB, 2006–2015), to cyber ranges and training automation under the NEC-endowed CROND chair (CyRIS/CyTrONE/CyLMS/CyPROM, 2016–2021), and then to CPS attack simulation (smart grid 2019–2022, smart building 2024–2026). From there it reaches RL agents that train on those ranges (AutoPentest-DRL 2020 → PenGym 2024 → CMARL-ACD 2026), and finally LLM security and LLM-for-security (2024–2026).

### Cited Findings
- 2006–2015, NICT Hokuriku / StarBED: QOMET (2007), QOMB (2009), 802.15.4 emulation (2011), fault injection on a large-scale testbed (AINTEC 2011 best paper), NICT award for wireless emulation (2012). — [Beuran CV](https://www.jaist.ac.jp/~razvan/cv.html); [Beuran publications](https://www.jaist.ac.jp/~razvan/publications.html)
- 2016–2021, CROND (NEC-endowed chair, closed March 2021): CyRIS (SoICT 2016), CyTrONE (ICISSP 2017, Interop 2017 finalist), CyLMS, CyPROM, IoTrain, CyATP, GridAttackSim, AutoPentest-DRL. — [crond-jaist repos](https://github.com/orgs/crond-jaist/repositories); [Beuran CV](https://www.jaist.ac.jp/~razvan/cv.html)
- 2021–present, Cybersecurity Research Lab (cyb3rlab): PenGym builds on CyRIS. Newer lines are LLM CTI analysis, domain LLMs, secure code generation, LLM jailbreak and safeguards, and smart-building emulation. The four lab directions are cybersecurity, IoT/CPS security, trustworthiness assurance, and education and training. — [cyb3rlab](https://github.com/cyb3rlab); [Beuran Lab page](https://www.jaist.ac.jp/is/labs/beuran-lab/index.html)
- The ETACS 2025 keynote "Cybersecurity Education and Training in Japan" and the 2025 Springer book consolidate the training work. The 2024–2025 talks are titled "Applications of AI/ML to Cybersecurity". — [Beuran publications](https://www.jaist.ac.jp/~razvan/publications.html)

### Inferences
- The pivot points line up with funding and institutions: NICT/StarBED → NEC-endowed CROND (training) → in-house lab with KDDI and NEC industry partners (RL and LLM).
- Smart-grid work (2019–2022) was largely driven by one PhD student and was not continued after Le graduated. The CPS-testbed thread resurfaced in 2024–2026 as smart-building emulation (SBCSE).

### Gaps
- Funding sources (KAKEN grants, NEC/KDDI contracts) were not verified.
- Whether StarBED itself is still used for the lab's cyber ranges after 2015 is unknown.
