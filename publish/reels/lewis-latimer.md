Edison invented the bulb. Lewis Latimer made it last.

Lewis Howard Latimer was born in Chelsea, Massachusetts, on September 4, 1848. His parents, George and Rebecca Latimer, had escaped slavery in Virginia six years earlier; his father's arrest in Boston had become one of the most famous fugitive slave cases of the time.

At sixteen, Lewis joined the Union Navy and served on the gunboat USS Massasoit in the last year of the Civil War. Back in Boston, he was hired as an office boy at a patent law firm. He watched the draftsmen, bought his own tools and books, taught himself mechanical drawing, and rose over the years to chief draftsman.

In 1876 he drew the patent drawings for Alexander Graham Bell's telephone.

In 1880, Hiram Maxim, Edison's rival at the United States Electric Lighting Company, hired him. The early light bulbs used a fragile carbon filament. In 1882 Latimer patented a better process for making it (U.S. Patent 252,386, "Process of Manufacturing Carbons"), and went on to supervise the installation of electric lighting in New York, Montreal and London.

In 1884 Edison's own company took him on as a draftsman and patent expert, and in 1890 he wrote "Incandescent Electric Lighting", which became a standard guide for lighting engineers. In 1918 he was named one of the 28 charter members of the Edison Pioneers, the only Black member.

He was also a poet, a painter and a musician, and taught English and mechanical drawing to immigrants in New York. He died in Flushing, Queens, on December 11, 1928, at 80. His house is now the Lewis Latimer House Museum.

Edison invented the bulb. Latimer made it last.

## Notes before posting

- "Edison invented the bulb" is the popular shorthand; many people contributed to the incandescent lamp. Latimer did not invent the bulb; his 1882 patent was a process for making the carbon filament.
- Sources differ on the year Edison's company hired him (1884 or 1885); 1884 (Lemelson-MIT, Wikipedia) is used.
- The two 1882 portraits exist only as small scans (about 350 px wide); they are upscaled and look soft at full screen. If a sharper public-domain scan turns up, swap it in.
- Not used: Philadelphia among the cities he lit (one source only); his 1874 railroad-car toilet patent; the air-conditioning forerunner.
