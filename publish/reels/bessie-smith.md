## Facebook

Post text:
She was the biggest blues star in America. Then she lay in an unmarked grave for 33 years.

Bessie Smith was orphaned as a child in Chattanooga and sang on street corners for coins. In 1923 Columbia Records signed her, and her first record, Downhearted Blues, sold about 780,000 copies in six months. She became the highest-paid Black entertainer of her day and toured in her own railroad car.

In 1927, when hooded Klansmen came to pull down her tent in North Carolina, she walked out to them alone, cursed them, and they ran. Then she went back in and finished the show.

She died after a car crash in Mississippi in 1937. Ten thousand people filed past her coffin. But her grave got no stone, until 1970, when Janis Joplin helped pay for one. Two months later, Janis was dead too.

Had you heard her story?
