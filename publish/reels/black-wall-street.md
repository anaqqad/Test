## Facebook

Post text:
They called it Black Wall Street. In less than a day, it was gone.

Greenwood, in Tulsa, Oklahoma, was built by Black families shut out of the white part of a segregated city. By 1921, about 10,000 people lived there, with newspapers, churches, doctors, lawyers and a movie theater, the Dreamland.

On June 1, 1921, armed white men crossed the tracks, looted homes and set them on fire. 35 city blocks burned. About 10,000 people lost their homes. No one was ever sent to prison for it.

Greenwood rebuilt, house by house.
