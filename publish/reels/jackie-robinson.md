## Facebook

Post text:
He broke baseball's color line. But three years before, the Army put him on trial for refusing to move to the back of a bus.

On July 6, 1944, at an Army base in Texas, Lieutenant Jackie Robinson refused a bus driver's order to move to the back. He was court-martialed, and a panel of nine white officers found him not guilty.

Three years later, on April 15, 1947, he walked onto the field for the Brooklyn Dodgers. He faced racial slurs from the dugouts, hate mail and death threats, and he did not fight back. That year he won baseball's first Rookie of the Year award.

Today every major league team has retired his number, 42.
