## Facebook

Post text:
He beat the Nazis' champion in 124 seconds. Then his own government came for everything.

Joe Louis was the grandson of enslaved people, the son of Alabama sharecroppers. In 1936 the German Max Schmeling knocked him out, and the Nazi regime made Schmeling a national hero.

On June 22, 1938, they met again at Yankee Stadium in front of 70,000 people, with millions listening on the radio in America and Germany. It was over in two minutes and four seconds.

Louis held the heavyweight title for almost twelve years and defended it 25 times. In 1942 he gave the purses from two title fights to the Navy and Army relief funds and joined the Army.

The government counted that money as his income. With interest and penalties, his tax debt passed one million dollars. He fought on into his late thirties, wrestled, and worked as a greeter at Caesars Palace.

When he died in 1981, President Reagan waived the rules so he could be buried at Arlington. Max Schmeling was a pallbearer.
