Post text:
She ran away from George Washington. And he never stopped hunting her.

Ona Judge was born enslaved at Mount Vernon and became Martha Washington's personal maid. In 1796 she learned she would be given away to Martha's granddaughter.

So on May 21, 1796, while the President ate dinner, she walked out the door. Free Black friends got her onto a ship to Portsmouth, New Hampshire. A newspaper ad offered ten dollars to bring her back.

Washington found her and tried to get her shipped back. She refused. In 1799 he sent his nephew to seize her, but a senator secretly warned her, and she hid.

That December, Washington died. She was never caught.

Asked if she regretted running, she said: "No, I am free."

She died in 1848. By law, she was still a fugitive.
