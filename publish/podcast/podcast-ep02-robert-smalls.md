Before dawn on May 13, 1862, an enslaved man put on the captain's straw hat and sailed an armed Confederate steamer past the guns of Fort Sumter, with sixteen people aboard.

Robert Smalls could not afford to buy his own wife and daughter. So he took the Planter, handed it to the U.S. Navy, piloted Union ships through the war, bought the house where he had been enslaved, and served five terms in Congress.
