Description (paste as is; Spotify turns the timestamps into chapters):

He was the most photographed American of the nineteenth century, and no, it wasn't Abraham Lincoln.

Frederick Douglass was born into slavery in Maryland around 1818. He learned to read in secret, fought back against the man sent to break him, and escaped in 1838 disguised as a sailor. He became one of the most powerful speakers and writers in American history, and he used the camera on purpose: about 160 portraits, to answer racist cartoons with dignity.

## Notes
