## Title

33 Years in an Unmarked Grave: The Bessie Smith Story

## Description

Bessie Smith sang for pennies on the street corners of Chattanooga. By the mid-1920s she was the Empress of the Blues, the highest-paid Black entertainer in America, touring the South in her own railroad car. She faced down the Ku Klux Klan alone, and made one film, St. Louis Blues, the only footage of her singing. Then the Depression took her career, a car crash on Highway 61 took her life, and her grave went without a headstone for 33 years, until Janis Joplin helped pay for one.
