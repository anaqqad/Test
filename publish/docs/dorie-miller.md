## Title

The Navy Made Him Serve Meals. At Pearl Harbor, He Manned a Machine Gun.

## Description

On December 7, 1941, Doris "Dorie" Miller was collecting laundry on the battleship USS West Virginia at Pearl Harbor. The Navy had put him in the messman branch, the place it kept Black sailors, to feed and serve the officers. Before the morning was over, he was firing a machine gun he had never been trained to use. The Navy praised him without giving his name, until a Black newspaper, the Pittsburgh Courier, found it. He became the first Black sailor to receive the Navy Cross, and he was still a mess attendant. This is his story.
