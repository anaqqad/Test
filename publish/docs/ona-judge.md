## Title

She Ran Away From George Washington. He Never Stopped Hunting Her.

## Description

In 1796, Ona Judge, enslaved by George and Martha Washington, walked out of the President's House in Philadelphia while they sat at dinner. She had just learned she would be given away to Martha's granddaughter. She escaped by ship to Portsmouth, New Hampshire. Washington spent the rest of his life trying to get her back: a runaway ad, the Treasury Department, a customs officer, and finally his own nephew. She refused every time, and she was never caught.
