## Title

She Won an Oscar. Hollywood Wouldn't Let Her Be Buried There.

## Description

In 1940, Hattie McDaniel became the first Black person ever to win an Oscar, and she accepted it from a segregated table against the far wall. The daughter of formerly enslaved parents, she went from a washroom attendant's job in Milwaukee to Gone with the Wind. She couldn't attend the film's Atlanta premiere, fought white neighbors in court to keep her home, and starred as Beulah on radio and TV. When she died in 1952, Hollywood Cemetery refused to bury her. Then her Oscar disappeared.
