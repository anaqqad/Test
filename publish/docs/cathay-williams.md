## Title

She Joined the Buffalo Soldiers Disguised as a Man

## Description

In November 1866, a recruit named William Cathay enlisted in the U.S. Army in St. Louis. William Cathay was a woman: Cathay Williams, born enslaved in Missouri, a Union army cook and laundress in the Civil War. She served almost two years in the 38th U.S. Infantry, one of the new Black regiments known as the Buffalo Soldiers, marching to forts in New Mexico, until a post surgeon found out. Years later, old and sick, she applied for a veteran's pension. It was rejected. She is the only woman known to have served as a Buffalo Soldier.

Every fact in this video comes from at least two reliable sources. No authenticated photograph of Cathay Williams is known; the soldiers shown are other Buffalo Soldiers, captioned.
