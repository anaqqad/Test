## Title

He Had to Black Up Every Night: The Bert Williams Story

## Description

Bert Williams was the highest-paid Black entertainer in America, the first Black man to star on Broadway, and the only Black star of the Ziegfeld Follies. And every night, before he walked on stage, he had to blacken his face with burnt cork. This is his story: from Nassau to In Dahomey and Buckingham Palace, from the Follies to the freight elevator, from his famous silent poker routine to the night he collapsed on stage in Detroit, and the audience thought it was part of the act.
