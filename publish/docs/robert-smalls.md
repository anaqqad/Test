## Description

In May 1862, Robert Smalls, an enslaved wheelman in Charleston, South Carolina, took the Confederate steamer Planter, picked up his family and sailed past five forts, including Fort Sumter, to the Union fleet. Sixteen people went to sleep enslaved and were free by sunrise. Then he helped persuade Washington to let Black men fight, captained the ship he had taken, served five terms in Congress, and bought the house where he had been enslaved.
