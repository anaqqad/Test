## Title

15 Forgotten Black Towns America Erased

## Description

They didn't fade away. They were burned, flooded, bulldozed and paved over. From a Georgia town under Lake Lanier to a Detroit neighborhood under a freeway, these are 15 Black towns and neighborhoods that America erased, counting down to the one most people think they know: Greenwood, Tulsa.

Every fact in this video comes from at least two sources. Where the sources disagree (death tolls, numbers of homes), we give a range or leave the number out.
