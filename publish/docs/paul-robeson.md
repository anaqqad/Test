## Title

America Took His Passport: The Paul Robeson Story

## Description

Paul Robeson was the son of a man who escaped slavery. He was a two-time All-American at Rutgers, a Columbia law graduate, a star of Show Boat and Othello, and one of the most famous singers in the world. Then he spoke out against lynching and segregation, and his own government took his passport. He was blacklisted, attacked at Peekskill, turned back at the Canadian border, and called before Congress, where he told them: "You are the un-Americans, and you ought to be ashamed of yourselves."
