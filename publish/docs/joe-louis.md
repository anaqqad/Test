## Title

He Beat the Nazis' Champion. Then America Taxed Him for Life: The Joe Louis Story

## Description

Joe Louis was the grandson of enslaved people and the son of Alabama sharecroppers. In 1938 he knocked out Max Schmeling, the Nazi regime's hero, in two minutes and four seconds in front of 70,000 people at Yankee Stadium. He held the heavyweight title for almost twelve years, a record, and in 1942 he gave the purses from two title fights to the Navy and Army relief funds. Then the government counted that money as his income. His tax debt passed one million dollars, and it followed him for the rest of his life.
