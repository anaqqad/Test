## Title

17 Million Letters. 855 Black Women. They Did It in Half the Time.

## Description

In February 1945, about 17 million letters and packages for American soldiers sat undelivered in warehouses in Birmingham, England. The Army expected the job to take six months. It sent 855 Black women of the Women's Army Corps, the 6888th Central Postal Directory Battalion, led by Major Charity Adams. They cleared it in three, under the motto "No mail, low morale," then did it again in France. They came home to no parade. In 2025, they received the Congressional Gold Medal.
