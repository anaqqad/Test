## Title

They Said Black Men Could Not Fly. Then They Escorted Bombers to Berlin.

## Description

In 1925, an Army War College study claimed Black men did not have what it takes to lead in the military, or to fly. Twenty years later, the Tuskegee Airmen, the Red Tails, were escorting American bombers to Berlin. This is the real story: Chief Anderson and Eleanor Roosevelt, Benjamin O. Davis Jr. and his four years of silence at West Point, the 1943 report that nearly ended the program, the true bomber record behind the legend, and the 101 Black officers arrested at Freeman Field in 1945.
