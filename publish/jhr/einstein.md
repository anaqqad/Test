He was the most famous scientist on Earth. The Nazis drove him out. Then the FBI kept a secret file on him, from 1932 until the day he died.

In 1952 Israel offered him its presidency. He said no.

- The "$5,000 bounty on his head": single source (Wikipedia, from a magazine claim); not spoken. Only the "not yet hanged" caption is spoken, with "reportedly".
- That his summer house became a Hitler Youth camp: sources disagree (Wikipedia/Forward vs Atlas Obscura: Wehrmacht, then residential).
- Antwerp vs Brussels consulate: sources disagree; the script says only "Belgium".
- FBI agents opening his mail and searching his trash: only one source found; dropped.
- Exact page count (1,427 vs 1,800): said as "more than fourteen hundred pages".
- The Commons film is labelled "arrives in America from Nazi Germany in 1933", but that date is not confirmed (it may be one of his earlier US arrivals, 1930-1932). No date is shown on the footage.
