## Facebook Reel caption

She was Hollywood's first vamp, sold to America as a desert temptress born in the shadow of the Sphinx. She was really a Jewish tailor's daughter from Cincinnati.

Theda Bara was born Theodosia Goodman in Cincinnati in 1885. In 1915, A Fool There Was made her a star and gave America the word "vamp". Fox's press agents said she was born in the Sahara and that her name was an anagram of "Arab Death". None of it was true. She made almost forty films for Fox, and most of them were lost in the 1937 Fox vault fire.

Had you heard of Theda Bara before?
