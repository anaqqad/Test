## Facebook Reel caption

He could escape from anything. Then a séance brought him a message from his dead mother, and he knew it wasn't her.

Harry Houdini was born Erik Weisz in Budapest in 1874, the son of a rabbi. In 1922, Lady Doyle, the wife of Arthur Conan Doyle, wrote out a "message" from his mother Cecilia. It began with a cross, and it was in fluent English, a language his mother had barely spoken. Houdini spent his last years exposing the mediums. He died on Halloween 1926 and was buried with his head resting on her letters.

Had you heard this part of his story?

Sources: Wikipedia; Library of Congress; PBS American Experience; History.com; The New York Times (Nov. 5, 1926).
