He was one of the best-paid stars on American radio. Then he stood up to a priest who was attacking Jews on the air, and it almost ended his career.

Eddie Cantor was born in New York in 1892 to Jewish immigrants from Russia and raised in poverty by his grandmother. He became a Ziegfeld star, a movie star and a radio star. In 1938 he raised hundreds of thousands of dollars to get Jewish children out of Nazi Europe. In 1939, at the New York World's Fair, he called out Father Charles Coughlin, the antisemitic "radio priest". His sponsor, Camel, dropped him.
