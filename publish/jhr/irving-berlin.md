## Facebook Reel caption

He wrote God Bless America. And the Ku Klux Klan called for a boycott, because the man who wrote it was a Jewish immigrant.

Irving Berlin was born Israel Beilin in the Russian Empire in 1888, the son of a poor cantor. His family fled the pogroms and landed at Ellis Island in 1893, when he was five. He grew up in a Lower East Side tenement, sold newspapers after his father died, and never learned to read music. He wrote "God Bless America" in an Army camp in 1918 and put it away for twenty years. When Kate Smith sang it on November 10, 1938, one day after Kristallnacht, America fell in love with it. Hate groups did not. His answer was to give it away: all its royalties go to the Boy Scouts and Girl Scouts.
