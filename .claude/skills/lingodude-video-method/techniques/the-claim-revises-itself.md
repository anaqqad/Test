# The Claim Revises Itself

**Origin:** Invented for Malta v3, after WWII Frontlines' ContextTag (stating what a picture is and is not).

**When to use:** Myths and over-simplifications ('never written down', 'comes from Phoenician'). The wrong claim appears, gets struck through when the evidence lands, and the corrected wording rises under it, with its source.

**How to build (`lingo/longform3.mjs`):**
`{ type: "claim", kicker, text, revised, source, at: 0.4–0.7, top }`. `at` is the fraction of the overlay when the strike happens; time it to the word in the narration that turns it around.

**Pitfalls:** Show uncertainty in the revised line itself ('Rarely written', 'roughly', 'counts vary').
