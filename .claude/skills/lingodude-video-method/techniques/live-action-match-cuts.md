# Live-Action Match Cuts

**Origin:** Built for Malta v4's bakery sequence (stand-in footage labelled on screen).

**When to use:** Word comparisons about everyday things (bread, book, God). Real hands and real sound carry the scene; the typography answers the recording.

**How to build (`lingo/longform4.mjs`):**
Cut on action every 1.5–2.5 s: hands working the dough → a real Maltese photo (baker, the loaf) → the word appears letter by letter as the native speaker says it (`words` overlay) → the oven → the Arabic word heard over the bread coming out. Location sound from the footage runs under all of it and is pre-lapped 0.5 s before the first cut. Label stand-in footage: `tag: "[ STAND-IN FOOTAGE · … ]"`.

**Pitfalls:** Never imply stand-in footage is Malta. Use real local photos for the claim itself.
