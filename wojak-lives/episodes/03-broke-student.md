*"I have twelve euros forty. And nineteen days to survive."* The HUD appears with a game "level start" sound.

"Day 1 of surviving on €12" as a game title screen.

## Full script

> **VO:** I have twelve euros forty. And nineteen days to survive.
> **VO:** Rule one: pasta. Pasta for breakfast. Pasta for lunch. Pasta for dinner is called "evening pasta."
> **VO:** Sauce? Sauce is for people with a future.
> **VO:** Rule two: never miss free food. This month I attended a seminar on tax law, a chess-club meeting, and a dentists' conference. I am not a dentist.
> **BRO:** Let's just split it equally!
> **VO:** Then, the most dangerous words in the world. I had tap water. Free tap water. Now I owe seventeen euros for someone's cheesecake.
> **MAMA (video call):** Are you eating well?
> **NOBO:** Yes Mama! Vegetables every day!
> **VO:** Ketchup is basically a salad.
> **VO:** Day fourteen. I searched the sofa like an archaeologist. Thirty-seven cents, a button, and a fry from 2021. I ate the fry.
> **VO:** Then, a miracle. Mama sent fifty euros. I am rich.
> **VO:** Did I buy food? Did I pay my debts? No. I bought a mechanical keyboard. Because I deserve nice things.
> **VO:** Anyway. Evening pasta.

## Virality
- **Why click:** a specific number in the title (€12.40, 19 days) creates a challenge frame.
- **Why keep watching:** the HUD is a live scoreboard. People stay to see the balance hit bottom and whether he survives.
- **Punchlines:** the split-the-bill beat (0:31) and "I am not a dentist" are the most quotable.
- **Audience:** students 16–26 worldwide and everyone who used to be a broke student (nostalgia sharing).
- **Shareable because:** "split it equally" is a universal friendship argument, so people send it to *that* friend.
- **Make the first 3 s stronger:** open on the ATM digits with a cash-machine beep and a "-" sign flicker, and say the number in the first 1.5 s.
