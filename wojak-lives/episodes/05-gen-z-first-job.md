## Full script

> **VO:** First day at my first real job. I googled "how to be an adult" in the elevator.
> **BOSS:** We're like a family here!
> **VO:** Red flag number one.
> **VO:** Then I learned corporate. "Per my last email" means "can you read." "Let's circle back" means "never." And "quick call" is never quick… and it's always a call.
> **VO:** The phone on my desk rang. A phone. With a cable. I answered like a grandma. "Hello? Who is this? How did you get this number?"
> **VO:** Meeting. One hour. Twelve people. It could have been an email. The email could have been a text. The text could have been a nod.
> **VO:** Five p.m. My contract says five p.m. So I stood up. The whole office looked at me like I'd stolen a printer.
> **BOSS:** Leaving already? Must be nice!
> **NOBO:** It IS nice! See you tomorrow!
> **VO:** In my generation, we call that reading the room, and leaving it.
> **VO:** Nine-thirty p.m. Email from my boss. "Quick question 🙂." That smiley has ended careers.
> **VO:** But I speak corporate now. I replied: "I'll look into it first thing tomorrow!"
> **VO:** One year later.
> **NOBO:** Welcome! We're like a family here!
> **VO:** …Oh no.

## Virality
- **Why click:** "Gen Z at work" is a perpetually trending debate topic; the title invites both Gen Z ("so true") and older workers ("they're so lazy"), which brings comments from both sides without being hostile.
- **Why keep watching:** the TRANSLATION strip is a promise of a fresh joke every few seconds; the "one year later" card is a curiosity spike near the end, which helps completion.
- **Punchlines:** stolen printer (0:40) and the twist (1:08).
- **Audience:** 18–30 first-jobbers, plus anyone in an office. Strong LinkedIn/Slack sharing ("this is our office").
- **Shareable because:** coworkers send it to each other, and the twist makes older viewers laugh too, since it isn't anti-boss, it's about becoming one.
- **Make the first 3 s stronger:** open on the search bar already typed with a "searching…" spinner, and say "first real job" within the first second.
