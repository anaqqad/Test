coming 🙂"**. His eyes pop (**BOING**). VO: *"Every introvert's horror movie starts with one text."*

**Stronger-hook options to A/B test:** (a) open on the bubble alone, full screen, with a horror-sting
sound, then reveal Nobo; (b) start on the line "My thumbs betrayed me." with the phone already showing
"haha sure!!" sent.

## Full script (VO = Nobo's inner voice; others speak in gibberish + bubbles)

> **VO:** Every introvert's horror movie starts with one text.
> **VO:** And my thumbs said "sure" before my brain could stop them.
> **VO:** Now I have six hours to prepare. Emotionally. Like an astronaut.
> **VO:** I practised saying "hey" in the mirror. Forty times. Version thirty-seven was too excited. They'd think I'm starting a cult.
> **VO:** I arrive. I stand outside the door for eleven minutes, listening to people having fun.
> **BRO (bubble):** YOU CAME!!!
> **VO:** Then the door opens and someone yells my name. Out loud. In front of witnesses.
> **VO:** I find my spot. Next to the snacks. Strategic. If anyone talks to me, I'm chewing.
> **ALEX (bubble):** Hi! I'm Alex!
> **NOBO (bubble):** Thanks, you too!
> **VO:** …"You too." He told me his name. Why did I say "you too."
> **VO:** So I did what any mature adult would do. I found the cat. Best conversation of the night.
> **VO:** At 10:04, I performed the introvert's greatest move: leaving without saying goodbye to anyone.
> **VO:** Home. Pyjamas. Silence. Perfect.
> **BRO (phone):** ur so fun!!! same time next week??
> **VO:** …They liked me. Being likeable is a trap.
> **VO:** And my thumbs said "sure."

## Animation instructions
- Nobo breathes in every shot (36-frame loop); blink every 3 s in shots 3, 9 and 11.
- Shot 2/13: thumbs = swap two hand positions every 2 frames (blur feel).
- Shot 4: the reflection is the same Nobo layer flipped horizontally at 85 % opacity inside the mirror area.
- Shot 6: Bro slides in from the right (8 frames, overshoot); 2-state mouth flap during the shout.
- Shot 8: Alex slides in from the left; a yellow **ALEX** label pops in next to him (an NPC becoming a person is a gag of its own).
- Shot 10: P13 tiptoe = 2 frames alternating every 6 frames while the layer moves left; NPC pupils slide with him.

## Editing instructions
- **Cuts:** hard cuts on VO sentence ends; no transitions except a whip pan from shot 5 to 6.
- **Zooms:** punch-ins at 0:01 (boing), 0:24 (door), **0:36 (you too, 130 %)**, 1:01 (text).
- **Text:** title tag the whole video; word-chunk captions; yellow keywords: TEXT, SURE, FORTY, ELEVEN, WITNESSES, CHEWING, YOU TOO, CAT, GOODBYE, TRAP.
- **Music:** cozy lo-fi; ducks under the muffled party bass in shots 5–10 (low-pass filter at 400 Hz = "behind a door"); cut music completely for the record scratch.
- **Timing:** one joke every ~6 s; never hold a frame over 3 s without a change.
- **Loop:** shot 13 is a frame-exact copy of shot 2's framing so the TikTok loop is seamless.

## Virality
- **Why click:** about a third of people call themselves introverts; the title is an identity badge.
- **Why keep watching:** a ticking situation (will he go?), with escalation every 5 s and the "you too" disaster as a promised payoff.
- **Punchlines:** 0:15 cult · **0:38 "you too"** · 0:45 cat · 0:52 Irish exit · 1:04 trap · 1:08 loop.
- **Audience:** 16–35, global, students and young workers.
- **Shareable because:** "this is literally you" tags between friends, and the "you too" moment is a universal cringe memory.
- **Make the first 3 s stronger:** put the phone bubble on screen at frame 1 (no build-up), and use a real horror sting so the joke is understood with the sound on *and* off.
