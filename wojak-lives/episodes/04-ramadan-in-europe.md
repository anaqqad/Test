## Full script

> **COWORKER:** Wait… not even water?
> **VO:** It's Ramadan in Europe. I've been asked this fourteen times. It's 9 a.m.
> **COWORKERS:** Not even water? / Not even gum? / Not even… a LITTLE water?
> **VO:** Water doesn't have a "little" setting.
> **VO:** Eleven a.m. Gary microwaves fish. Every Ramadan. Gary has never microwaved fish in any other month.
> **VO:** Twelve-thirty. Surprise birthday cake. "Oh, sorry, you can't, right?" And then she eats it. Slowly. Maintaining eye contact.
> **VO:** Three p.m. People think you lose weight in Ramadan. No. You gain three kilos. Every iftar is a wedding.
> **VO:** Five p.m. Grocery shopping while fasting. My brain says: four pizzas, a cake, and a melon the size of a child.
> **VO:** Six-twelve. The moment. I've waited all day. I will eat everything.
> **VO:** One date. Half a soup. Full. The pizzas look at me with disappointment.
> **VO:** Then I sleep three hours and wake up at 3:30 to eat again. Like a very spiritual raccoon.
> **VO:** Next day, Gary brings dates.
> **GARY:** I googled it. I'm fasting with you today!
> **VO:** Gary. Respect.
> **VO:** …He lasted until eleven.

## Virality
- **Why click:** "Not even water?" is the most famous Ramadan meme, so Muslims recognise it instantly and non-Muslims are curious.
- **Why keep watching:** the iftar countdown is a ticking clock; the viewer wants to reach sunset with him.
- **Punchlines:** eye contact (0:24), one date (0:50), and Gary's 11 a.m. (1:10) are the three biggest.
- **Audience:** Muslims in Europe/North America (tens of millions), plus their coworkers and friends, who share it *to* their Muslim colleague. That cross-community share is the growth engine.
- **Shareable because:** it's affectionate to both sides. Muslims feel seen, and coworkers feel included (Gary is the hero, briefly).
- **Make the first 3 s stronger:** start with the NPC's line already on screen at frame 1 and the counter jumping to ×14 with a slot-machine sound.
