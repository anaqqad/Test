# Long-form research: what works for Black history on YouTube (2026-11-22)

Why: our 8-minute single-person docs get 6-242 views each (Bert Williams 8, Bessie Smith 8, Lena Horne 36,
Joe Louis 36, Paul Robeson 119, Hattie McDaniel 220, Ona Judge 16, James Armistead 242, Black Wall Street 24).

Method: YouTube search (16 queries, long-video filters, last-year uploads), about 500 videos collected; kept
Black-history videos with 150k+ views; then looked at the channels behind them.

## Patterns

1. Length: 20-60 min (lists) or 1-3 h (deep dives / "for sleep"). Nothing near 8 min in the winners.
2. A theme across many people, a place, or a group, not one lesser-known person. Single-person videos win only
   with a huge name (Malcolm X, Bumpy Johnson, Toussaint Louverture) or a shocking angle.
3. Numbered lists: "14 Forgotten Black Towns", "19 Black Old West Legends", "10 Black Men Who Passed as White",
   "25 Black Actors Hollywood Won't Cast".
4. Hooks that repeat across winners:
   - Hidden Black wealth: millionaires, mansions, elite, rich suburbs, Black plantation owners.
   - Destroyed or erased places: towns flooded for lakes, Central Park (Seneca Village), highways, Black Wall Street.
   - Passing as white / families that "turned white".
   - Fighting back against the KKK.
   - Old West: Black cowboys and lawmen (Bass Reeves).
   - "They never taught you" / "you were lied to" framing.
5. Titles: plain and concrete, number + "forgotten/silenced/secret" + a clear subject. Thumbnails: one face or
   a place + 3-5 words.

## Ideas for The Black History Room (long-form)

- 15 Forgotten Black Towns America Erased (flooded, burned, bulldozed) - 30-40 min list
- The Black Millionaires America Forgot (Annie Malone, Madam C.J. Walker, Robert Reed Church, Mary Ellen Pleasant...) - 40 min
- 10 Black Families Who Disappeared Into White America (passing) - 35-40 min
- The Black Men Who Fought the Klan - 30-40 min
- The Real Lone Ranger and 15 Black Legends of the Old West - 25-30 min
- Hollywood's First Black Stars, and What Hollywood Did to Them (reuse our film-clip work: Hattie McDaniel,
  Lena Horne, Paul Robeson, Bert Williams, Bessie Smith) - 40 min compilation from existing material
- Seneca Village: the Black Town Under Central Park - 25-30 min single-place story
