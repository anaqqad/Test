# Website addendum (owner, 2026-10-15): content structure and the Global African History map

Read together with `docs/tasks/2026-10-14-wordpress-blog.md`. This file changes the content model and adds one
feature. Everything else in the main brief (Docker, n8n safety, security, SEO, backups, drafts-only automation) stays.

## What the site is

Mainly a library of short, sourced stories about people and events. Inspiration: BlackPast.org's structure
(an encyclopedia of entries grouped by region and subject, a "Global African History" section with a country
map, cards with photo, name, years, short excerpt, "Read more"). **Do not copy BlackPast's design, text, images or
code.** Use our own brand (black / cream / deep red / muted gold, archival editorial look) and our own content.

## Two main sections, each with subcategories by domain

1. **African American History**
2. **Global African History** (Africa and the African diaspora: Caribbean, Latin America, Europe, etc.)

Subcategories (same list under both sections, so they can be filtered together or separately):
Politics & Government · Civil Rights & Activism · Slavery & Emancipation · Military · Science, Medicine &
Invention · Business & Economics · Arts, Music & Literature · Sports · Religion · Education · Women in History ·
Exploration · Law & Justice.

Recommended data model (no heavy plugins; register in a small custom plugin or mu-plugin in our repo):
- Post type **Story** (one person or event per entry; normal posts can stay for news/blog).
- Taxonomies: **Section** (African American / Global African), **Domain** (the list above), **Country**
  (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code as the slug, e.g. `ma` for Morocco, `gh` for Ghana), **Era** (by century), optional **Tag**.
- Fields on each Story: full name, birth year, death year (blank if living), short excerpt (~40 words),
  featured image (public domain / CC0 / licensed, with credit), sources list (at least two reliable sources),
  related reel / podcast episode link. Use native custom fields or a tiny meta box; avoid ACF Pro.

URLs: `/african-american-history/<domain>/<slug>/`, `/global-african-history/<country>/<slug>/` (or a simpler
`/story/<slug>/` with breadcrumbs; pick one and keep it stable).

## Listing pages

- Card grid like the screenshot: image (square crop), name + years, two-line excerpt, "Read more", date.
  3 columns on desktop, 1 on mobile, lazy-loaded images, no carousel.
- Filters on each section page: Domain, Country, Era; A–Z index of names.

## The map feature (Global African History)

- Page `/global-african-history/` shows a world map. Countries that have at least one Story are coloured
  (deep red); countries without stories are dark grey and not clickable. Hover / focus shows the country name and
  number of stories.
- Clicking a country opens `/global-african-history/<country>/`, the Country archive: a short country intro
  (optional) and the card grid of that country's Stories. If a country has exactly one Story, it may link straight
  to it.
- Beside the map, a scrollable **alphabetical country list** (like the screenshot) with the same links. On phones,
  show the list first and the map below (or a "View map" button); the list is also the accessible, no-JS fallback.
- Build: a lightweight SVG world map rendered client-side from public-domain Natural Earth boundaries
  (world-atlas TopoJSON, 110m resolution, ~100 KB) with a small script (D3-geo or plain SVG paths pre-rendered at
  build time). Load the map script **only on that page**. Country colours come from a small JSON endpoint or an
  inline JSON list of country codes with story counts, generated by WordPress from the Country taxonomy.
- Zoom + / – buttons like the screenshot are optional; keyboard focus and screen-reader labels are required.
- Do not use a paid map plugin or a Google Maps API key.

## Editorial rules (unchanged)

Every Story needs at least two reliable sources and clearly separates fact, interpretation, disputed claims and
legends. Images must be public domain / CC0 or properly licensed, with credits. n8n creates Stories as **drafts**
only; the owner reviews and publishes.
