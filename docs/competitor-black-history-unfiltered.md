# Competitor: Black History Unfiltered (checked 2026-11-16)

## Numbers
- YouTube @Blackhistoryunfiltered: 456k subs, 1,124 videos, 144.3M views, joined 30 Jun 2025 (~2 uploads/day). About 2.4M views/week now.
- Facebook page: 1.1M followers ("They tried to erase it. We're bringing it back"), Buy Me a Coffee link.

## What gets views
- YouTube Shorts drive everything. Top Shorts: Wesley Snipes' Black Panther 4.5M; Ali's wife (Belinda) 4.4M; "She died listening to a song made just for her" 2.1M; tortured 93 days 2.1M; "He divorced her, then kept writing her love songs" 2.0M; Minnie Riperton 1.9M; "The #1 song MTV refused to play" 1.8M; Vanity 1.7M; Natalie Cole "Unforgettable" 1.5M; Sammy Davis Jr. love story Kennedy tried to erase 1.1M; George Jefferson's body frozen 4 months 1.0M; Nat King Cole 358k.
- Long videos (3-5 min, 16:9): only 4k-18k views each. Not the growth engine.
- Facebook reels: same videos as the Shorts (same thumbnails/edit). Last ~100 reels: most 10k-50k, top 100k-233k.
- Text posts (long article, Black military firsts) get 8k-15k reactions.

## Format of a Short / Reel
- Length: 2:50-3:50 (Shorts up to 3:00; FB reels 3:06-3:46). Not 60 s.
- Subject: famous Black entertainers (actors, singers, athletes, comedians) + a hidden/tragic/romantic angle: "Hollywood buried...", "the wife they erased", "died before his big break", "million dollar mistake".
- Visuals: real photos and movie stills of the star, warm sepia grade, slow push-ins, big semi-transparent logo watermark in the middle, small logo top right.
- Captions: 2-4 words at a time, white, current word highlighted yellow, lower third.
- Voice: AI narrator; Facebook shows "AI content" under their name (they label it).
- Title = the hook, 4-8 words, no hashtags. Description opens with a 1-2 sentence teaser.

## Risk for us
Their images are copyrighted (Blade stills, press photos, Getty-type portraits). Our rule is public domain / CC0 only. To copy the formula safely, pick stars from roughly 1920s-1970s, whose studio/TV publicity photos are often public domain on Wikimedia Commons (published without copyright notice). Modern stars (Snipes, Vanity, D'Angelo) mostly have no PD photos.
