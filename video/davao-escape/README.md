# The Davao escape: ten American POWs, the Bataan story and the gag order (WWII Frontlines)

On 4 April 1943 ten American prisoners and two Filipino convicts walked out of the Davao Penal Colony on Mindanao, the only
successful group escape from a Japanese POW camp in the Pacific war. Ed Dyess, McCoy and Mellnik reached Australia by the
submarine Trout; at home the War Department silenced them (Roosevelt's memo on the Gripsholm), Dyess died in a P-38 at Burbank
on 22 December 1943, and the story was released on 28 January 1944. Main source: John D. Lukacs, *Escape from Davao* (2010);
script in `script.md`, sources in `research/sources.md`.

time segments; 76 overlay cards in one pass ran out of memory). Spoken subscribe ask at 51 %. Thumbnails: `python tools/thumbs_episode.py`.
