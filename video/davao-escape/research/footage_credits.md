# Footage and photo credits (Davao escape episode)

All footage is shown in black and white. No film exists of the escape, of Dapecol in 1943, of Dyess's crash or of the
newsrooms in 1944; every film shot of those is a stand-in and is tagged on screen. No film of atrocities is shown.
