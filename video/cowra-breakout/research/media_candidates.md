# Media candidates: the Cowra breakout, 5 Aug 1944 (with Toyoshima, Darwin, Hardy and Jones, Hay, Cowra today)

Research date: 24 Nov 2026. This file is metadata only: no media was downloaded into the project.

Where the figures come from:
- AWM: read from each record page (the `mediaData` JSON and the "Item copyright" line).
- Commons: from the API `imageinfo` / `extmetadata`.
- archive.org: from `https://archive.org/metadata/<id>`.
- One 2-page AWM PDF was opened in the scratchpad to confirm page numbers, then deleted.

Rights key:
- **AWM "Copyright expired - public domain"**: the line printed on the AWM record. Australian Crown copyright on photos taken before 1955 has expired.
- **Commons licences**: as stated on the file page. CC BY and CC BY-SA files need an on-screen or description credit ("Photo: <author>, CC BY 3.0").
- **"Licence unclear - do not use"**: the holder does not state a free licence, or US/Japanese status is doubtful.

**AWM image size warning.** Every AWM photo below is offered online only as a ~640 px "Low Res" download. No high-res download is offered on awm.gov.au; "Order a copy" is a paid service, so ask before using it. The AWM's new test site (`https://collection.awm.gov.au/<C-id>`) has a "Deep zoom" (DZI) viewer for these records, which may serve the full scan. Its tiles at `assets.awm.gov.au` returned HTTP 403 to scripts, so the size is **unverified**: open one in a normal browser and check before relying on it. On 1080p, use 640 px AWM prints as framed "prints" or "contact sheets" in house style, not full-bleed.

The FELO series (AWM 067172-067205, 1-2 Jul 1944) is the set of Cowra photos taken **five weeks before the breakout** for the Far Eastern Liaison Office, as a basis for propaganda leaflets to be dropped over Japanese-held territory. The caption on every one says so.

## 2. FILM

Nothing on Cowra itself survives as public-domain film online. The AWM film catalogue has no 1944 footage of No. 12 camp, and the AWM's Cowra documentaries (F03365, F03334, F03376) are 1980s productions marked "External/Unlicensed copyright", so they are not usable. The plan is to tell the camp with stills and use film for:
(a) Japanese POWs in Allied camps, being captured or interrogated;
(b) Japanese naval aviators and carriers, for the Toyoshima thread;
(c) Darwin;
(d) the Senjinkun mindset;
(e) Australian rural and army texture.

Sizes and resolutions come from `https://archive.org/metadata/<id>`. The download URL is `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<file>`. None of these was downloaded or reviewed frame by frame: the shot order is NARA's own shot list, and there are no timecodes unless stated.

Rights key:
- **PD-gov**: US Navy (428-NPC), Signal Corps (111-ADC) or OWI work.
- **PD-JP**: a Japanese organisational newsreel from 1942. Its term expired in Japan before 1996, so it is not restored in the US. Captured copies are held by NARA and AWM.
- **PD-AWM**: the AWM record says "Copyright expired - public domain".
