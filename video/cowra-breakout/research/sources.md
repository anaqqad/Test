## Main sources (numbers as in facts_raw.md)

   30-ft towers; "constant wrestling and baseball"; Timms's "secret fear" quote; the Hay decision and 24 hours' notice;
   the 2 a.m. warning, bugle and warning shot; three mobs; the weapons list; ~400 at the north-west; Hardy and Jones;

## Disagreements (how the script handles them)
- Took part / got out: script says "more than a thousand" lay awake, "hundreds" came over the fence, "several hundred"
  got clear, 334 brought back in nine days (25 dead). No single "escaped" figure.
- Dead: 231 (court of inquiry) and "three more later died of their wounds", giving the official history's 234.
- Australians: four killed, plus Sgt Hancock, accidentally shot during the search (named separately).
- Bugle time: "a few minutes before two" / "about two"; the echo says 1:30 "is said to have begun".
- Who was to go to Hay: "all the Japanese, except the officers and the non-commissioned officers" (Long).
- The vote: "a scrap of paper ... One account says it was toilet paper"; circle yes, cross no.
- Three mobs (Long) used; not "four groups".
- The gun: both versions told, attributed (Long: jammed; Gordon: belt; Cooper "I think" and AWM: Hardy disabled it).
- Toyoshima's death: the grave record's wording (gunshot wound to the chest and a self-inflicted wound).
- Toyoshima's capture: Ulungura's own words (wrist, revolver, "Stick 'em up"); stick/tomahawk anecdotes not used.
- Cover story: "the records ... give two different versions"; no rank or number on screen.
- "Only Japanese war cemetery in Australia" (not "outside Japan").
- "Largest prison break of the Second World War": Smithsonian [38]; "only land battle ... on Australian soil": DVA [23].
- Not used (unverified): "No. 2 Vickers", the Ichiro Takata quote, Tokyo Rose broadcast, Murakami's "ghost" quote,
  the Darwin death toll (AWM page not reachable), the distance from Sydney.
