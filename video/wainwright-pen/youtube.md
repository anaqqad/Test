## Description
On September 2, 1945, aboard the USS Missouri, General Douglas MacArthur called two names before he signed the Japanese surrender. A thin, white-haired general stepped forward and stood behind him. Halfway through his signature, MacArthur stopped, turned, and handed him the pen. Three years earlier, MacArthur had told Washington that giving this same man the Medal of Honor would be "a grave injustice."

This is the documented story of General Jonathan "Skinny" Wainwright: the man MacArthur left behind on Corregidor, the surrender of 6 May 1942, the radiograms MacArthur sent about him, three years in prison camps from Luzon to Manchuria believing America blamed him, the OSS team that found him, the reunion in Yokohama, the pens on the Missouri, and the Medal of Honor President Truman gave him on September 10, 1945.
