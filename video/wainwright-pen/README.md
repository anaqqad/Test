# Wainwright and the pen (WWII Frontlines)

General Jonathan Wainwright, left on Corregidor by MacArthur in March 1942, surrendered the Philippines on 6 May 1942,
was called "unbalanced" and refused the Medal of Honor by MacArthur that year, spent three years in camps from Luzon to
Manchuria fearing disgrace, and was handed the first pen at the Japanese surrender on 2 Sep 1945. Title and thumbnail
follow the reference channel's "Japan Expected MacArthur to …" series. Script in `script.md`, sources in `research/`.
