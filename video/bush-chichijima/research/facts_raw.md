# Fact sheet (chronological): Bush's Navy file and the Chichi Jima airmen

Numbers in [brackets] refer to `sources.md`. P1–P7 are the primary documents in its last table. "UNVERIFIED" means only one source, or the sources conflict.

Times: the VT-51 report uses zone -9 (GMT+9). Finback's report uses zone King (GMT+10), so add 1 hour to VT-51 times to compare.

## 2 September 1944: Strike "Baker" on the Chichi Jima radio station
| Time | Fact | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| — | Crew: pilot Lt(jg) G.H.W. Bush (file 173464); Lt(jg) William G. "Ted" White (squadron ordnance officer, flying in place of turret gunner Leo Nadeau); ARM2c John L. Delaney (radioman). Aircraft TBM-1C BuNo 46214. | [P1 p.28], [P3 p.46], [22 46214] |
| 0715 (-9) | Four VT-51 TBM-1Cs take off from San Jacinto, each with four 500-lb GP bombs. Escort: 12 F6F-5 of VF-20 and 8 SB2C of VB-20, from USS Enterprise. | [P1 p.27] |
| 0825–0830 (-9) | Over the target: four buildings and a tower (radio station), plus a second radio station. The four planes in order: Lt Cdr D. J. Melvin (CO), Lt(jg) D. W./D. H. West, Bush third, Ens. M. G. Moore. "Very intense, heavy and medium A/A fire". | [P1 p.29–30] |
| — | Bush is hit in the engine "shortly after final push over at 8,000 ft". The engine smokes and catches fire. He "continued in his dive, releasing his bombs on the radio station… to score damaging hits" (released at about 4,000 ft). | [P1 p.29–30], [14 DFC citation] |
| — | He turns east toward the sea, with "smoke and flames enveloping his engine and spreading aft", radios the CO that he must bail out. About 9 miles bearing 045° from Minami Jima, "Bush and one other person were seen to bail out from about 3,000 ft"; the other chute "did not open". | [P1 p.30] |
| — | Bush strikes the tail on bailing out ("I pulled the ripcord too early"), lands in the water, inflates his raft and paddles away from Chichi Jima. VF-20 fighters circle overhead. | [P1 p.30], [17] |
| 0933 (King) | Finback receives word of a "plane down 9 miles NE of MINAMI JIMA" and rounds the southern end of Chichi Jima, staying at least 7½ miles off. | [P2 img 0632] |
| **1156 (King)** | **Finback picks up Bush. He "stated that he failed to see his crew's parachutes and believed they had jumped when plane was still over CHICHI JIMA, or they had gone down with plane."** Finback starts a search for the crew. In raft about 2.5 hours by the documents; "about four hours" in popular accounts. TM1c Donnet Kohler helps him aboard. The rescue is filmed in 8 mm from the conning tower; cameraman UNVERIFIED. | [P2], [P4 186382], [22], [26]; Disagreements 6 and 13 |
| 1236–1620 (King) | Second rescue under shore fire off Haha Jima: Ens. James W. Beckman (VF-20) is towed clinging to the periscope ("Spirits of all hands went to 300 feet"). | [P2 img 0632] |
| after 1620 | Beckman says "only one man had parachuted from BUSH's plane". The search for White and Delaney is called off. | [P2 img 0633] |
| same day | Bush's logbook: "46214 / 1.7 / Delaney, Lt(jg) White / CRASH LANDING IN SEA NEAR BONIN ISL. – ENEMY ACTION." | [P3 p.46] |
| 3 Sept (approx.) | Bush writes to his parents from the sub: "There was no sign of Del or Ted anywhere around"; "I sat in my raft and sobbed for a while"; he felt "so terribly responsible for their fate". | [19], [20] |

| Date | Fact | Sources |
|---|---|---|

| 25 Feb 1945 | Dye is executed by Capt. Shizuo Yoshii. | [22] |

| 15 Mar 1945 | Vaughn is beheaded (Pacific Wrecks, Navy Memorial). His body is cremated and the ashes scattered at sea. Nobuaki Iwatake, his guard and friend, later takes the name "Warren". | [22 F4U 82356], [24 Iwatake], [17] |
| 23 Mar 1945 | Tachibana is promoted lieutenant general; his brigade becomes the 109th Division. | [24 Tachibana] |

| Mar–May 1945 | Bush trains with VT-153 in Maine and Virginia for the invasion of Japan. | [24], [P3 p.49–58] |

## 1946–1951: investigation and trials (U.S. Navy Military Commission, Guam)
| Date | Fact | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| 7 Jan 1946 | Maj. Yoshitaka Horie admits his first statement was false (the airmen did not die in air raids). | [8] |
| 14 Jan, 6 and 8 Mar 1946 | Maj. Sueo Matoba testifies. He admits 8–10 executions and taking part in cannibalism three times: "Yes, I was a madman due to the war…" | [8] |
| 27 Mar 1946 | Medical officer Tadashi Teraki leaves a suicide note and disappears. | [8] |
| Early Aug 1946 | *U.S. v. Tachibana et al.* opens on Guam with 14 defendants. Charges: murder, violation of the laws of war, neglect of duty. 38 specifications, including **"prevention of honorable burial"**, because no law covered cannibalism. | [8], [9], [24 Tachibana] |
| Early Sept 1946 | Matoba's confession is read in court and reported by *Time* (16 Sept 1946, "Unthinkable Crime"). | [11] |
| 1946 | Verdict: 13 of 14 guilty. Tachibana and two officers sentenced to death; ten get 5 years to life. A separate case covers Vice Adm. Kunizo Mori and Matoba for five airmen killed in Feb 1945. Mori gets life on Guam and is later sentenced to death by a Dutch court (date UNVERIFIED). | [8], [9], [24] |
| 24 Sept 1947 | Tachibana is hanged at Asan-Maina, Guam, and buried in an unmarked grave. *Navy News* (26 Sept 1947) reports five executed. Matoba is hanged; the others named vary (Yoshii, Ito, Nakajima). | [24 Tachibana], [10], [22]; Disagreement 11 |
| 7 Aug 1948 | Teraki is arrested in Tokyo, living under the name "Maeda Tadashi". | [8] |
| 4–17 Mar 1949 | Teraki is tried and sentenced to 4 years for mutilating bodies. He is acquitted of a beheading. | [8] |
| 25 Jan 1951 | Teraki is paroled from Sugamo. | [8] |

## Named people

### American airmen and sailors
- **George Herbert Walker Bush.** Lt(jg) USNR, file no. 173464. Pilot, VT-51, USS San Jacinto. Born 12 Jun 1924, Milton MA; raised in Greenwich CT. Shot down 2 Sept 1944, rescued by Finback. Later 41st President. Died 30 Nov 2018. [P1–P3], [22]
- **John L. "Del" Delaney.** ARM2c USNR, service no. 2049043. Bush's regular radioman, VT-51. Hometown Rhode Island (Pacific Wrecks code "RI"); town UNVERIFIED. The middle name "Lawrence" is UNVERIFIED. Missing in action 2 Sept 1944, declared dead 3 Sept 1945. On the Courts of the Missing, Punchbowl, court 5. Posthumous Purple Heart. [P1 p.28], [22]
- **William Gardner "Ted" White.** Lt(jg) USNR, O-210562. VT-51 ordnance officer. Born 25 Feb 1918, Minnesota. Yale 1942. He flew in Nadeau's place to observe the island. Missing in action 2 Sept 1944, declared dead 3 Sept 1945. Courts of the Missing, court 2. Parents Edwin and Anna (weak source). [P1 p.28], [4 NAID 557766862], [22], [28]
- **Leo W. Nadeau.** Bush's regular turret gunner, VT-51. Not on the 2 Sept flight. Interviewed by CNN in 2003. [4], [17], [22]
- **Joe Reichert.** Radioman in a Bush "X-2" crew photo. [4]
- **D. J. Melvin.** Lt Cdr USNR. Commanding officer of VT-51, led the strike, approved the AAR. Credited with direct hits. First name commonly given as Don/Donald; UNVERIFIED. [P1]
- **D. W. (or D. H.) West.** Lt(jg) USNR, VT-51. Second plane over the target. [P1]
- **M. G. Moore.** Ens. USNR, VT-51. Fourth plane over the target. [P1]
- **"Kilpatrick" (reading uncertain).** Lt USNR. Prepared the AAR. [P1 p.31]
- **Jim Wykes.** Bush's roommate on San Jacinto (VT-51). Lost with his crew on a search mission around the Wake Island strike, May 1944. [5]
- **Chester Mierzejewski.** Of Cheshire, Conn. VT-51 turret gunner in a plane about 100 ft ahead of Bush's. DFC for 25 Oct 1944. Disputed Bush's account in 1988. [16], [17]
- **Lou Grab, Stan Butchart.** VT-51 comrades; signed the 1984 logbook page. Grab was interviewed by CNN in 2003. [P3 p.64], [17]
- **Robert R. Williams Jr.** Lt Cdr USN. CO of USS Finback from 2 Aug 1944. Signed the patrol report. [P2]
- **J. L. Jordan.** Lt Cdr USN. Previous CO of Finback. [P2]
- **Donnet Kohler.** TM1c, Finback. Pulled Bush aboard. [22], [26]
- **Finback officers in the 30 Sept 1944 photo:** Lt James Griswold, Lt(jg) William H. Parkman, Lt(jg) Jack Peat, Ens. William Edwards, LCDR Dean Spratlin, LCDR Robert Williams, Lt J. L. Lawrence, Lt Lawrie Heyworth, Lt Jerry Redmond, Lt(jg) Ollie Brostrom. Edwards and Spratlin signed the 1984 logbook page. [P4], [P3 p.64]
- **James W. Beckman.** Ens. USNR, file 301442. F6F pilot, VF-20, USS Enterprise. Rescued off Haha Jima on 2 Sept 1944, towed on the periscope. Reported that only one man parachuted from Bush's plane. [P2]
- **Thomas Keene** (Ens. USNR, file 298972), **James T. Stovall** (AOM3c USNR) and **John R. Doherty/Dougherty** (ARM3c USNR). VT-13, USS Franklin, plane T-11. Rescued by Finback on 1 Sept 1944 near Tobiishi Bana. Keene signed the 1984 logbook page. [P2], [P4], [P3 p.64]

### The eight airmen murdered on Chichi Jima
- **Lloyd Richard "Dick" Woellhof.** AR2c USNR, 6286511. Gunner, VB-1, USS Yorktown, SB2C-1 01169. From Clay Center, Kansas. Captured 4 Jul 1944; killed about 5–7 Aug 1944 (bayonet practice, beheaded by Lt Col Kikuji Ito on Tachibana's order). Body exhumed and cremated 16 Aug [1944 or 1945]. Courts of the Missing, court 2. His pilot, Ens. Owen M. Hintz (Oelwein, Iowa), died in the crash. [22]
- **Warren Arthur "Hindy" Hindenlang.** Ens. USN(R). Born 21 Oct 1920, Foxboro MA. Reportedly copilot of a VB-109 PB4Y-1 lost on a night raid 4/5 Aug 1944. Reported beheaded. Capture and execution date UNVERIFIED. [23], [24]
- **Grady Alvan (or Alvah) York Jr.** AOM3c, 5568232. Gunner, VT-82, USS Bennington, TBM-3 22904. From Jacksonville FL. Captured 18 Feb 1945; executed 23 Feb 1945 at a rifle range by Capt. Masao Yamashita. Courts of the Missing, court 2. [22]
- **James Wesley "Jimmy" Dye Jr.** ARM3c. Radioman on the same plane as York. From Mount Ephraim NJ. Captured 18 Feb 1945; executed 25 Feb 1945 by Capt. Shizuo Yoshii. Buried at Punchbowl, section N, grave 1291. [22]
- **Floyd Ewing Hall.** Ens. USNR. Pilot, VT-12, USS Randolph, TBM-3 23637. Hometown Sedalia MO per a weak source; UNVERIFIED. Captured 18 Feb 1945; executed 9 Mar 1945 on Maj. Matoba's order. Reported cannibalized. Buried at Santa Fe National Cemetery (group burial with Frazier and Mershon, Section N, grave 275). [22], [28]
- **Glenn J. Frazier Jr.** AOM2c USNR. Gunner on Hall's plane. From Athol KS. Hid for five days, then surrendered. Beaten to death by Capt. Noburu Nakajima on 24 Feb 1945 (or 18 Feb). [22]
- **Marvelle "Marve" William Mershon.** ARM3c USNR. Radioman on Hall's plane. From Los Angeles (weak source). Beheaded 24 Feb 1945 by Lt Hironobu Morishita on Matoba's order. Reported cannibalized. [22], [28]
- **Warren Earl Vaughn.** 2nd Lt USMCR, O-029620. Pilot, VMF-123, USS Bennington, F4U-1D 82356. From Childress TX. Shot down and captured 23 Feb 1945; beheaded 15 Mar 1945 (or 9 Mar). Cremated, ashes scattered at sea. Courts of the Missing, court 4. Posthumous Air Medal and Purple Heart. [22], [24 ja]
- **Robert T. King** (Ens., pilot of 22904, survived) and **Robert J. Cosbie** (Ens., pilot of TBM 22889, which collided with 22904). [22]

### Japanese
- **Yoshio Tachibana.** Born 24 Feb 1890, Ehime. Maj Gen, then Lt Gen (23 Mar 1945). Commanded the 1st Independent Mixed Brigade, later the 109th Division, on Chichi Jima. Ordered the executions and the destruction of records. Tried Aug 1946; hanged 24 Sept 1947, Guam. [8], [24]
- **Sueo (Sueyo) Matoba.** Major, CO of the 308th Independent Infantry Battalion ("Tiger of Chichi Jima"). Ordered executions and cannibalism; confessed. Hanged on Guam, 1947. [8], [11], [22]
- **Kunizo Mori.** Rear Adm., then Vice Adm. (May 1945). Commander of the Chichi Jima naval base force. Life imprisonment on Guam; later sentenced to death by a Dutch court for crimes in the Dutch East Indies (date UNVERIFIED). [9], [24]
- **Shizuo Yoshii.** Captain IJN, commander of the naval communications unit. Executed Dye; reported to have eaten organs. Hanged on Guam (most sources). [22], [24]
- **Kikuji Ito.** Lt Col IJA. Killed Woellhof. Hanged in 1947 (Guampedia, Pacific Wrecks). [10], [22]
- **Noburu Nakajima.** Capt. IJA. Beat Frazier to death. Pacific Wrecks says he was hanged; UNVERIFIED elsewhere. [22]
- **Masao Yamashita.** Capt. Executed York. Prison sentence. [22]
- **Hironobu Morishita.** Lt. Beheaded Mershon. Prison sentence of "several years". [22]
- **Yoshitaka Horie.** Major, Tachibana's chief of staff. First gave the false air-raid story, then recanted. Later wrote memoirs (*Tōkon Iōjima*). [8], [24 ja]
- **Bunji Enomoto.** 1st Lt, 308th Battalion. Testified that Matoba staged the cover story. [8]
- **Tadashi Teraki.** Probationary medical officer. Removed organs; fled; arrested 1948; 4-year sentence; paroled 1951. [8]
- **Nobuaki "Warren" Iwatake.** Born 1923, Kahului, Maui. IJA radio operator on Chichi Jima. Vaughn's guard and friend. Met Bush on Chichi Jima in June 2002. Died 2012. [17], [24]
- **Kimitoshi Tsuchiya.** Midshipman on Chichi Jima, later president of the Japan Federation of Bar Associations. Disputed the "banquet" story. [24 ja]

### Investigators, lawyers and others
- **Presley M. Rixey.** Col. USMC. Led the Chichi Jima board of investigation (from 21 Dec 1945) and the prosecution. [8], [22]
- **John D. Murphy.** Captain, later Rear Adm. USN. Director of War Crimes, Pacific Fleet. [9], [10]
- **Thomas L. Gatch.** Rear Adm. Judge Advocate General of the Navy. [9]
- **Frederick Arthur Savory.** Bonin islander, descended from early Western settlers. His reports first alerted U.S. investigators. [11]
- **James Bradley.** Author of *Flyboys* (2003). Interviewed Bush in 2002. [17], [21]
