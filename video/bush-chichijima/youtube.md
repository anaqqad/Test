## Description
```
On 2 September 1944, Lt.(jg) George H.W. Bush of Torpedo Squadron 51 (VT-51), USS San Jacinto, was shot down over Chichi Jima. His Navy file shows what happened, and where its own pages disagree.

We read the file page by page: the squadron's secret Aircraft Action Report, his own flight logbook, the war patrol report of the submarine USS Finback that pulled him from the sea, his Distinguished Flying Cross citation, and the U.S. Navy war crimes trial on Guam that revealed what happened to the eight American airmen captured on Chichi Jima. You will see the real film of his rescue and hear Bush himself, from White House recordings of 1991 and 1992.
```
