# The German nurses returned through the lines, Normandy, July 1944 (WWII Frontlines)

German Red Cross nurses and navy auxiliaries captured at Cherbourg were shown through the 45th Evacuation Hospital and
returned to German lines at Caumont under a radio truce on 2 and 9 July 1944, as the 1929 Geneva Convention required.
Script in `script.md`, sources in `research/sources.md`, treaty pages in `research/docs/`.

First episode built at 1080p with lighter encoding (no added grain or denoise, crf 18/17): `assemble.py --hd`, cards with
`HEIGHT=1080`. Spoken subscribe ask at the midpoint with the `SubscribeButton` overlay. Thumbnails: `python tools/thumbs_episode.py`.
