## Description
Early in 1984, a woman named Jean packed her bags and called a taxi. Her husband of twenty years, a ski instructor and tennis pro called Dennis Whiles, would not talk about his past. So he told her: his real name was Georg Gärtner, and he was a German soldier who had escaped from an American prisoner-of-war camp in 1945.

This is the documented story of Hitler's last soldier in America: why a prisoner from Silesia was afraid to go home when the war ended, the night he crawled under the fences at Camp Deming, New Mexico, and climbed into a Southern Pacific boxcar, the forty years he spent as someone else, and the day in 1985 he walked into an immigration office in San Pedro and gave himself up.
