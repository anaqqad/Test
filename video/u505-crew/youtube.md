## Description
In the summer of 1944, the families of 58 German submariners were told their men must be considered dead. Their boat, U-505, was long overdue. In fact the men were alive, in an isolated corner of Camp Ruston, Louisiana, with their names kept from the Red Cross and their letters confiscated.

On June 4, 1944, an American hunter-killer group under Captain Daniel Gallery forced U-505 to the surface off West Africa, boarded her as she circled with her valves open, and took her codebooks and Enigma machine. The capture was only worth something if Germany believed the boat had sunk. "We keep our mouths shut about it," Gallery wrote to his men. This is the documented story, told from the Navy's own reports, the captain's statement, the Top Secret memo and the record after the war.
