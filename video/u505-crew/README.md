# U-505's crew, hidden at Camp Ruston (WWII Frontlines)

Captured off West Africa on 4 June 1944, U-505 was only useful if Germany believed she had sunk with all hands, so her 58
survivors were held in isolation at Camp Ruston, Louisiana, kept from the Red Cross, and listed as dead at home until after the
war. Script in `script.md`, sources in `research/sources.md`, Gallery's Top Secret memo in `research/docs/`.
