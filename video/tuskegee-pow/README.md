# Alexander Jefferson, Tuskegee Airman and POW, 1944-45 (WWII Frontlines)

A Tuskegee pilot shot down near Toulon on 12 August 1944 meets a German interrogator whose book already holds his
school grades and his father's tax bill; nine months in Stalag Luft III and Stalag VII-A, "no segregation, no
discrimination"; Dachau the day after liberation; then a sign at the bottom of a New York gangplank. Script in
`script.md`, sources in `research/sources.md` (Public Law 109-213 in `research/docs/`).
