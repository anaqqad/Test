# The Salina shooting (WWII Frontlines)

On July 8, 1945, two months after V-E Day, an American guard machine-gunned the tents of sleeping German prisoners at Salina,
Utah: nine dead, nineteen wounded, no trial. Told from the 1945 Utah newspapers. Script in `script.md`, sources in
`research/sources.md`, footage in `research/footage_credits.md`.

The four 1945 newspaper images in `assets/stills/` were captured from Utah Digital Newspapers; the rest come from `tools/fetch_stills.py`.
