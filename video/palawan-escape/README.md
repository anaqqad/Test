# The Palawan massacre and escape: 150 American POWs, 11 survivors (WWII Frontlines)

On 14 December 1944 the guards of the prison camp at Puerto Princesa, Palawan, sounded an air raid alarm, forced the 150
American prisoners into their shelters and set them on fire with gasoline. Eleven escaped, through the fence and down the cliff,
and most swam the five-mile bay; Filipino convicts at Iwahig and Palawan guerrillas hid them until flying boats took them out in
January 1945. Built on the War Crimes Branch report of 18 Aug 1945 and the survivors' sworn statements (Tokyo tribunal records,
scans from Japan's National Diet Library), the Signal Corps photo album of the camp and Stephen L. Moore's *As Good as Dead*.
Script in `script.md`, sources in `research/sources.md` (primary scans and OCR in `research/docs/`).

Built at 1080p: `assemble.py --only-base --hd` for the clips, cards with `HEIGHT=1080 bash tools/render_missing.sh`, then
`python tools/assemble_parts.py 3`. Seven LedgerDocument cards highlight the 1945 documents (`tools/doc_spans.json`).
Spoken subscribe ask at 55 %. Thumbnails: `python tools/thumbs_episode.py`.
