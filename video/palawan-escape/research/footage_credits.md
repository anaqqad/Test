# Footage and photo credits (Palawan episode)

All footage is shown in black and white. No film exists of the massacre or the escape. The film of the camp, its shelters and
the bay was shot by the Signal Corps after the landing of 28 February 1945; every other shot of the sea, the jungle, the flying
boats and the prisoners is a stand-in and is tagged on screen. No image of the dead is shown (the Signal Corps excavation
photos in album 2985, NARA 531317 and the 1945 "burial site" photo were left out on purpose).
