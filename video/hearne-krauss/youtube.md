## Description
In 1943, a family from New York City traveled to a prison camp in the cotton country of Texas to see their son. He was a corporal in the German army. They brought him a sewing machine and a radio. On the night of December 17, a group of German prisoners walked into his barracks with clubs and spiked boards. The attack lasted less than a minute.

Hugo Krauss was born in Germany and grew up in Yorkville, Manhattan. He went back to Germany in 1939, ended up in the German army, fought in Russia and North Africa, and returned to America as a prisoner of war at Camp Hearne, Texas. He spoke perfect English, worked as an interpreter, and told the others he wanted to stay. This is the documented story of how his fellow prisoners killed him, how his father wrote to the Army, and how the case was solved two years later, because one of the killers stopped on a Texas road to fix a young woman's car.
