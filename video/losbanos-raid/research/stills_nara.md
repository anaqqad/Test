# NARA stills (public domain, U.S. Army Signal Corps)
Source: NARA 80668173, 111-SCA Album 2895 "Philippine Islands - Luzon - Le to Lu" (https://catalog.archives.gov/id/80668173)
- report_p1_1945.jpg (album p.71): "Rescue of Internees from Los Banos Internment Camp" p.1, extracted from 188th Glider Infantry Narrative History, Luzon, 31 Jan-30 Jun 1945, pp. 9-10. Key lines: task force "Los Banos Force" under Col. Robert H. Soule; units list incl. "52 Amtracks", "9 C-47s, 54th TC Wing"; "The date of striking the target was 0700 on 23 February"; "Company B, 511th jumped on the target at H-hour, surprised the enemy garrison at morning calisthenics and annihilated them"; "The day prior to the operation the Division Reconnaissance Platoon had landed at Los Banos under the cover of darkness"; "completely finished by 1330"; "every one of the 2200 prisoners was liberated and evacuated to the Muntinlupa Hospital."
- report_p2_1945.jpg (album p.72): from 11th Airborne Division, Report after action with the Enemy, Luzon Campaign, pp. 6, 7, 59. "camp 25 miles behind enemy lines on the south shore of Laguna de Bay"; "more than 2000 American and Allied Nationals and an estimated 8000 enemy troops were in the immediate area"; "evacuated in Amtracs to Muntinlupa across Laguna de Bay by 1700 the same day"; "only one internee was injured"; "internees were of 6 nationalities, of both sexes, of ages from 6 months to 70 years, and 240 were litter cases requiring medical care."
- grave_hell_1945.jpg + caption_hell_1945.jpg (p.75/76): SC 269120-R "Jan Howard Hell, American civilian whom the Japanese killed 18 May 1945 [sic, likely error for Jan.], is buried here. Los Banos, Luzon, P.I. 5/18/45", SWPA-SC-45-15623 (Sgt H. Guyon).
- grave_louis_1945.jpg + caption_louis_1945.jpg (p.77/78): SC 269119-R "George James Louis, American civilian whom the Japanese killed 28 January 1945, is buried here. Los Banos, Luzon, P.I. 5/18/45", SWPA-SC-45-15622.
- caption_ang_1945.jpg (p.114): SC 210419 "Filipino children Elisa Ang, aged six, and her older brother, bearing marks of bayonet wounds, are the only survivors of the Ang family; nineteen others were killed by Japs at Los Banos, Luzon, P.I. 5/19/45". (Photo p.113 and p.79 show the children partly undressed: do NOT show; caption card only.)
