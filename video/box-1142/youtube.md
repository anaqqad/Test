## Description
During the Second World War, not far from the Pentagon, German prisoners sat in small rooms and talked: U-boat officers, army officers, scientists. In another building, young American soldiers in headphones wrote it all down. Many of them had been born in Germany or Austria, and many were Jewish refugees who had escaped the Nazis only a few years before.

This is the documented story of P.O. Box 1142, the secret interrogation center at Fort Hunt, Virginia: the hidden microphones and stool pigeons, the chess games and steak dinners, the fake Red Army officers, what the listeners learned about Peenemünde and the U-boat pens, the U-boat commander shot on the fence, the escape kits mailed to American prisoners in Germany, and the veterans who kept the secret for sixty years.
