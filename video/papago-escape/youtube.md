## Description
On the night of December 23, 1944, twenty-five German prisoners of war crawled out of Camp Papago Park, Arizona, through a 178-foot tunnel they had dug by hand. Three of them carried a folding boat in pieces. Their plan was to paddle it down the Salt, the Gila and the Colorado rivers to Mexico.

They had hidden the dirt in their pockets and on a faustball court the guards were happy to let them build. They had saved food for weeks, forged papers and waited for Christmas. What they had not seen from inside the wire was the river.

This is the documented story of the largest escape of enemy prisoners from an American camp in the Second World War: the U-boat captains who planned it, the "three mad boatmen", the greatest manhunt in Arizona history, and the last man out, Captain Jürgen Wattenberg, who spent a month in a cave and was caught in Phoenix with seventy-five cents.
