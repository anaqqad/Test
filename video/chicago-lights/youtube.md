## Description
In September 1945, German prisoner of war Reinhold Pabel walked away from a small branch camp in Washington, Illinois. He caught a bus in Peoria with $3.75 left in his pocket and rode through the night toward Chicago.

He came from a country that had been blacked out since 1939. At the edge of the city, he later wrote, "a million lights pop up everywhere."

This is his documented story: captured in Italy in 1943, landed at Norfolk in January 1944, held at Camp Grant and Camp Ellis, then almost eight years hiding in plain sight as "Phil Brick", Chicago dishwasher and bookseller, until an FBI agent walked into his shop in 1953.
