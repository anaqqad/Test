**Title**
German POWs Came From a Blacked-Out Continent. Then One Rode Into Chicago at Night.

(Alternative, closer to the channel's winning pattern: "A German POW Escaped in 1945. Then He Saw Chicago's Lights.")

**Description**
In September 1945, German prisoner of war Reinhold Pabel walked out of a small branch camp at Washington,
Illinois, caught a bus in Peoria, and rode through the night toward Chicago. He came from a country that had
been blacked out since 1939. At the edge of the city, he later wrote, "a million lights pop up everywhere."

This is his documented story: captured in Italy in 1943, landed at Norfolk in January 1944, held at Camp Grant
and Camp Ellis, then eight years in hiding as "Phil Brick", Chicago dishwasher and bookseller, until the FBI
walked into his shop in 1953.
