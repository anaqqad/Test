# Media candidates: Japanese surrender, 15 Aug - 2 Sep 1945

Research date: 23 Nov 2026. Metadata only; no large file was downloaded. Sizes, resolutions and durations come from the
archive.org metadata API (`https://archive.org/metadata/<id>`), the NARA catalog proxy API
(`https://catalog.archives.gov/proxy/records/search?naId=<NAID>`) and, where marked "probed", from an `ffprobe` header
read of the remote file (no download). Shot lists are NARA's own (quoted in the archive.org descriptions or read from the
NARA "ADC Shot List Card" PDFs, 0.1 MB each).

Download URL patterns:
- archive.org: `https://archive.org/download/<identifier>/<file name>`
- NARA direct: the `catalog.archives.gov/medialz/...` URL given in the row.

Rights key:
- **PD-gov**: US Army / Navy / Signal Corps / AAF / OWI "United News" work, public domain. NARA puts a blanket
  "Restricted - Possibly / Copyright" flag on the whole 111-ADC series and on United News reels because the series
  also holds commercial newsreel material. The shot cards below show these specific reels are Army Pictorial Service /
  AFCF camera footage, so treat them as US government works.
- **PD-Univ**: Universal Newsreel on archive.org (donated to the public domain).
- **Unclear**: commercial newsreel (Castle Films, British Paramount, Periscope reissues) or an uploader's licence on an
  AI-enhanced copy. Avoid, or use only after checking.

### Notes on films

- **MacArthur's voice on film (US government sources):** #1 MN-9034G (opening speech), #2 111-ADC-5163 (closing
  "These proceedings are closed"), probably #4 United News 39079 and #10 Screen Magazine 68. Real POW voices: #32
  ADC-5281 and the card-only 111-ADC-5162 (see below). Every other ceremony reel is silent camera original.
- **Best Atsugi footage is NOT online.** These NARA Signal Corps reels have shot cards but no digitised video in the
  catalog (a NARA reproduction order would be needed):
  - 111-ADC-762, "Landing at Atsugi Airstrip", 30 Aug: C-54s massed, US flag raised on a hangar, released POWs
    interviewed, Gen. Arisue, "**MacArthur smoking corn cob pipe stands in doorway of C-54**", greeted by Eichelberger
    and Swing, "excellent CUs".
  - 111-ADC-5149: MacArthur arrives smoking his pipe, comes down the steps, talks with Eichelberger, Sutherland and
    Akin; Japanese photographers.
  - 111-ADC-5150: arrival of dignitaries, "excellent CUs" of MacArthur with Eichelberger and Kenney, cockpit view of
    the landing.
  - 111-ADC-5160, 2 Sep: **MacArthur leaves the New Grand Hotel** and gets into a car, boards ship, is greeted by Halsey
    and Nimitz, flyover, Percival aboard, Wainwright and Percival leave ship.
  - 111-ADC-5162, 1 Sep: liberated POWs interviewed at the Imperial Hotel, Tokyo (SOUND): Bataan, hell-ship coal bins,
    walking out of Camp Maibara.
  - Also card-only: 111-ADC-760, 5153, 5154, 5156, 5158, 5159, 5161, 5164 (V-J Day Yokohama), 5239 (retreat parade at
    the Grand Hotel GHQ, flag over the US Embassy).
  - Online substitutes for MacArthur at Atsugi: #9 United News 39078, #10 Screen Magazine 68, #39 Appointment in Tokyo.

## Gaps

1. **MacArthur's landing at Atsugi**: the best Signal Corps reels (111-ADC-762, 5149, 5150, including the pipe in the C-54
   doorway) are not digitised online. Online coverage is newsreel (39078, Screen Magazine 68, Appointment in Tokyo).
   Stills: Spaarnestad scan, 3098 px. The NHHC colour USA C-1732 is only 740 px on Commons.
2. **The drive to Yokohama and the Hotel New Grand**: no online film (111-ADC-5160 is card only). Only one 800 px 1945
   still (Truman Library 98-2447). Use the ruins reels (NPC-14671/14673, CS-9405 Yokohama harbour) for the drive.
3. **Wainwright and Percival arriving in Yokohama, 31 Aug**: no film found. Stills: the New Grand meeting (111-SC-210621)
   and the 80-G-3541xx Yokohama series (N1-N4). Film exists of the Manila stop (111-ADC-5188).
4. **B-29 supply drops**: no moving footage found; strong stills (N5, N6, N10).
5. **Kase**: no stand-alone 1945 portrait; he can be identified in the group shots.
6. **The Bataan C-54**: no PD photo of the aircraft itself.
7. **Original sound of "Today the guns are silent"**: only on the NBC network recording (rights unclear). Government
   film soundtracks give the opening and closing ceremony words.
8. **General Order No. 1**: no stand-alone scan found.
9. **Kosco colour film** of the ceremony: found online only as an AI-upscaled NC-licensed copy. The original should be
   sought at NARA.
